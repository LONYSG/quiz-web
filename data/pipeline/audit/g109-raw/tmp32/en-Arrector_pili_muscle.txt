The '''arrector pili muscles''', also known as '''hair erector muscles''',<ref name=":4">{{Cite web|title=Anatomy of the Skin {{!}} SEER Training|url=https://training.seer.cancer.gov/melanoma/anatomy/|access-date=2021-01-21|website=training.seer.cancer.gov}}</ref> are small muscles attached to [[hair follicles]] in [[mammals]]. Contraction of these muscles causes the hairs to stand on end,<ref name="Cormack2001">{{cite book|author=David H. Cormack|title=Essential histology|url=https://archive.org/details/essentialhistolo0000corm_p5i3|url-access=registration|access-date=15 May 2011|date=1 June 2001|publisher=Lippincott Williams & Wilkins|isbn=978-0-7817-1668-0|pages=[https://archive.org/details/essentialhistolo0000corm_p5i3/page/1 1]–}}</ref> known colloquially as [[goose bumps]] (piloerection).<ref name=":0">{{Cite journal|date=2011-02-18|title=The Basement Membrane of Hair Follicle Stem Cells Is a Muscle Cell Niche|url= |journal=Cell|language=en|volume=144|issue=4|pages=577–589|doi=10.1016/j.cell.2011.01.014|issn=0092-8674|last1=Fujiwara|first1=Hironobu|last2=Ferreira|first2=Manuela|last3=Donati|first3=Giacomo|last4=Marciano|first4=Denise K.|last5=Linton|first5=James M.|last6=Sato|first6=Yuya|last7=Hartner|first7=Andrea|last8=Sekiguchi|first8=Kiyotoshi|last9=Reichardt|first9=Louis F.|last10=Watt|first10=Fiona M.|pmid=21335239|pmc=3056115}}</ref>

== Structure ==
Each arrector pili is composed of a bundle of [[smooth muscle]] fibres which attach to several follicles (a follicular unit).<ref name=":1" /> Each is innervated by the sympathetic division of the [[autonomic nervous system]].<ref name=":1">{{Cite journal|last1=Pascalau|first1=Raluca|last2=Kuruvilla|first2=Rejji|date=August 2020|title=A Hairy End to a Chilling Event|journal=Cell|volume=182|issue=3|pages=539–541|doi=10.1016/j.cell.2020.07.004|pmid=32763185|s2cid=221012408|issn=0092-8674|doi-access=free}}</ref> The muscle attaches to the follicular stem cell niche in the follicular bulge,<ref name=":0" /><ref name=":1" /><ref name=":2">{{Cite journal|last1=Torkamani|first1=Niloufar|last2=Rufaut|first2=Nicholas|last3=Jones|first3=Leslie|last4=Sinclair|first4=Rodney|date=2017-01-01|title=The arrector pili muscle, the bridge between the follicular stem cell niche and the interfollicular epidermis|url=https://doi.org/10.1007/s12565-016-0359-5|journal=Anatomical Science International|language=en|volume=92|issue=1|pages=151–158|doi=10.1007/s12565-016-0359-5|pmid=27473595|s2cid=26307123|issn=1447-073X|url-access=subscription}}</ref> splitting at their deep end to encircle the follicle.<ref name=":3">{{Cite journal|last1=Poblet|first1=Enrique|last2=Jiménez|first2=Francisco|last3=Ortega|first3=Francisco|date=August 2004|title=The contribution of the arrector pili muscle and sebaceous glands to the follicular unit structure|url=https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jaad.2004.01.054|journal=Journal of the American Academy of Dermatology|volume=51|issue=2|pages=217–222|doi=10.1016/j.jaad.2004.01.054|pmid=15280840|issn=0190-9622|url-access=subscription}}</ref>

== Function ==
The contraction of the muscle is involuntary. Stresses such as [[cold]], [[fear]] etc. may stimulate the [[sympathetic nervous system]], and thus cause muscle contraction.<ref name=":1" />

=== Thermal insulation ===
Contraction of arrector pili muscles have a principal function in the majority of mammals of providing thermal insulation.<ref name=":1" /> Air becomes trapped between the erect hairs, helping the animal retain heat.

=== Self defense ===
Many animals experience contraction of the arrector pili muscle in response to a perceived threat. This helps the animal seem larger and more intimidating as a result.<ref>{{cite journal |last=McPhetres |first=Jonathon |last2=Zickfeld |first2=Janis H. |title=The physiological study of emotional piloerection: A systematic review and guide for future research |url=https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0167876022001556#bb0155 |journal=International Journal of Psychophysiology |publication-date=September 2022 |volume=179 |pages=6-20 |doi=10.1016/j.ijpsycho.2022.06.010 |via=Elsevier Science Direct}}</ref>

=== Sebum excretion ===
Pressure exerted by the muscle may cause [[sebum]] to be forced along the hair follicle towards the surface, protecting the hair.<ref>
{{Cite journal |last=Poblet |first=Enrique |last2=Jiménez |first2=Francisco |last3=Ortega |first3=Francisco |date=2004-08-01 |title=The contribution of the arrector pili muscle and sebaceous glands to the follicular unit structure |url=https://www.jaad.org/article/S0190-9622(04)00556-0/abstract |journal=Journal of the American Academy of Dermatology |language=English |volume=51 |issue=2 |pages=217–222 |doi=10.1016/j.jaad.2004.01.054 |issn=0190-9622}}</ref>

=== Hair follicle stability ===
Arrector pili muscles also stabilise the base of the [[hair follicle]].<ref name=":2" /><ref name=":3" />

=== Clinical significance ===
Skin conditions such as [[leprosy]] can damage arrector pili muscles, preventing their contraction.<ref>{{Cite journal|date=2010-09-01|title=Histopathological changes in the arrector pili muscle of normal appearing skin in leprosy patients|journal=International Journal of Infectious Diseases|language=en|volume=14|pages=e70–e72|doi=10.1016/j.ijid.2009.11.018|issn=1201-9712|last1=Budhiraja|first1=Virendra|last2=Rastogi|first2=Rakhi|last3=Khare|first3=Satyam|last4=Khare|first4=Anjali|last5=Krishna|first5=Arvind|pmid=20207571|doi-access=free}}</ref> Inducing contraction of the arrector pili muscles via an [[Alpha-1 adrenergic receptor|α1-adrenergic receptor]] agonist has been shown to reduce hair shedding as a result of [[traction alopecia]].<ref>{{cite journal |last=Goren |first=Andy |last2=Shapiro |first2=Jerry |last3=Sinclair |first3=Rodney |last4=Kovacevic |first4=Maja |last5=McCoy |first5=John |date=December 17, 2015 |title=α1-AR agonist induced piloerection protects against the development of traction alopecia |url=https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/abs/10.1111/dth.12324 |journal=Dermatologic Therapy |doi=10.1111/dth.12324 |via=Wiley Online Library|url-access=subscription }}</ref>

== History ==
The term "arrector pili" comes from Latin. It translates to "hair erector".<ref name=":4" />
