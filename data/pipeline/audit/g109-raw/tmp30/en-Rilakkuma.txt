{{nihongo|'''Rilakkuma'''|リラックマ|Rirakkuma}} is a fictional character produced by the Japanese company [[San-X]] and created by [[Aki Kondo]]. San-X portrays Rilakkuma as an [[anthropomorphized]] teddy bear close to his bear friends Korilakkuma and Chairoikoguma, along with a bird Kiiroitori. After the character's debut in 2003, Rilakkuma has been featured on [[stationery]] and [[merchandise]] created by San-X, as well as multiple collaboration cafès. Along with picture books and [[comics]], a [[stop-motion animation]] series titled ''[[Rilakkuma and Kaoru]]'' was broadcast in 2019.

An anime adaptation started airing in April 2026, the series is animated by the studio [[Production I.G]].<ref>{{cite web|title=Rilakkuma Short Form TV Anime Debuts in April 2026|url=https://www.crunchyroll.com/news/latest/2025/8/25/rilakkuma-short-form-tv-anime-debuts-in-april-2026|work=Crunchyroll|date=August 25, 2025}}</ref>

==Development==
[[San-X]] employee [[Aki Kondo]] had previously created characters such as [[Amagurichan]] and [[Mikanbouya]].<ref name="japantimes 2019-04-18">{{cite news | first=Patrick | last=St. Michel | url=https://features.japantimes.co.jp/rilakkuma/ | title=Bear essentials: Rilakkuma's adorable stop-motion debut | work=[[The Japan Times]] | date=2019-04-18 | access-date=2019-05-17}}</ref> At the time, San-X ordered staff members to create one character per month.<ref name="japantimes 2019-04-18"/><ref name="yahoo 2006">{{cite web |url=http://books.yahoo.co.jp/interview/detail/31354549/01.html | script-title=ja:子どものインタビュー一覧 コンドウアキ | language=ja | work=[[Yahoo!|Yahoo! Japan]] | date=2006-11-11 | access-date=2008-09-16 | url-status=dead |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20081205065355/http://books.yahoo.co.jp/interview/detail/31354549/01.html | archive-date=2008-12-05 }}</ref> After watching a TV show about [[dog]]s at the height of the pet boom in [[Japan]], Kondo not only wanted a pet dog, but also envied dogs' lives, as she was busy with work and hoped to relax more.<ref name="yahoo 2006"/><ref name="japantimes 2019-04-18"/> This inspired her to create Rilakkuma, with his name a [[portmanteau]] of the words {{nihongo|''relax''|{{Wikt-lang|ja|リラックス}}|rirakkusu}} and {{nihongo|''bear''|{{Wikt-lang|ja|クマ}}|kuma}}<ref name="japantimes 2019-04-18"/> Rilakkuma was designed with a slightly sloppy posture because Kondo found it amusing, believing that he should look "cute", yet "out of place."<ref name="japantimes 2019-04-18"/> Kondo had also designed Rilakkuma with a zipper on his back, which became one of his quirks.<ref name="japantimes 2019-04-18"/> San-X initially found Rilakkuma too gangly and had preferred characters with small hands.<ref name="yahoo 2006"/><ref name="japantimes 2019-04-18"/>

==Character==

Rilakkuma was launched in 2003 and first seen in San-X's [[Yonkoma|four-panel comic]] series titled ''Rilakkuma Seikatsu''.<ref name="japantimes 2019-04-18"/> In ''Rilakkuma Seikatsu'', which describes his origin story, he appears one day in the apartment of a female office worker named Kaoru, who only appears as a silhouette.<ref name="profile">{{cite web | url=http://www.san-x.co.jp/rilakkuma/profile/ | script-title=ja:リラックマのこと。 | language=ja | work=[[San-X]] | access-date=2019-05-17 | url-status=live | archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20151028153129/http://www.san-x.co.jp/rilakkuma/profile/ | archive-date=2015-10-28}}</ref><ref name="japantimes 2019-04-18"/><ref>{{cite news | first=Liz | last=Ohanesian | url=http://www.laweekly.com/arts/cute-japanese-character-rilakkuma-hits-los-angeles-with-parties-at-japanla-umami-burger-2374275 | title=Cute Japanese character Rilakkuma hits Los Angeles with parties at Japan LA, Umami Burger | work=[[LA Weekly]] | date=2010-08-17 | access-date=2019-05-17}}</ref> Rilakkuma spends every day relaxing.<ref name="profile"/> The zipper on his back reveals that his appearance is a costume, and his true form is unknown.<ref name="profile"/><ref>{{cite news | first=Rafael Antonio | last=Pineda | url=https://www.animenewsnetwork.com/news/2019-04-16/rilakkuma-and-kaoru-stop-motion-anime-special-video-reveals-costume/.145781 | title=Rilakkuma and Kaoru Stop-Motion Anime's Special Video Reveals 'Costume' | work=[[Anime News Network]] | date=2019-04-16 | access-date=2019-05-17}}</ref> It is accompanied by other creatures in the series, who also have their own character lines.<ref name="japantimes 2019-04-18"/>

===Korilakkuma===
'''{{nihongo|Korilakkuma|コリラックマ||{{literal translation}} "Little relaxing bear"}}''' is a white bear introduced in ''Rilakkuma Seikatsu'' in 2003.<ref name="japantimes 2019-04-18"/> Like Rilakkuma, it appeared in Kaoru's apartment one day and was named by Kiiroitori.<ref name="profile"/> It has a red button on its chest and does not appear to be a real bear.<ref name="profile"/> It has a mischievous personality and loves pranks.<ref name="profile"/>

===Kiiroitori===

'''{{nihongo|Kiiroitori|キイロイトリ||{{literal translation}} "Yellow bird"}}''' is Kaoru's pet yellow bird introduced in ''Rilakkuma Seikatsu'' in 2003.<ref name="japantimes 2019-04-18"/> He can leave his cage at any time and takes care of both Rilakkuma and Korilakkuma.<ref name="profile"/>

===Chairoikoguma===

'''{{nihongo|Chairoikoguma|チャイロイコグマ||{{literal translation}} "Brown little bear"}}''' is a brown honey bear introduced in 2016.<ref name="san-x chairoikoguma">{{cite web | url=https://www.san-x.co.jp/rilakkuma/campaign/knf/ | title=San-Xネット リラックマ「コリラックマと新しいお友達」テーマ | language=ja | work=[[San-X]] | access-date=2019-05-17}}</ref><ref>{{cite web | url=https://prtimes.jp/main/html/rd/p/000000004.000018631.html | script-title=ja:リラックマがクリスマスツリーに!? リラックマ「森のクリスマスツリーソファぬいぐるみ」サンエックスネットショップ限定受注販売スタート  | language=ja | work=PR Times | date=2016-08-26 | access-date=2019-05-17}}</ref> Korilakkuma finds it one day in the forest and befriends it, bringing it to meet Rilakkuma and Kiiroitori.<ref name="san-x chairoikoguma"/> Chairoikoguma has defined fangs when its mouth is opened, as well as bear-shaped honey patches on its feet and rear.<ref name="san-x chairoikoguma"/> Its friend is '''{{nihongo|Kumanbachi|くまんばち||{{literal translation}} "Bear bee ([[Carpenter bee]])"}}''', a honey bee.<ref name="san-x chairoikoguma"/>

==Marketing and distribution==
[[File:クレーンゲーム (9175467292).jpg|thumb|A [[claw machine]] with plush Rilakkuma toys]]
Along with stationery and product lines, Rilakkuma has collaborated with multiple organizations, brands,<ref>{{cite news | first=Vlad | last=Savov | url=https://www.engadget.com/2009/07/13/bandai-rilakkuma-netbook-suffers-from-serious-supercuteitis/ | title=Bandai Rilakkuma netbook suffers from serious supercuteitis | work=[[Engadget]] | date=2009-07-13 | access-date=2019-05-17}}</ref> and character cafes.<ref>{{cite web | url=https://tower.jp/site/rilakkuma/2017/cafe | title=リラックマ × TOWER RECORDS CAFE | language=ja | work=[[Tower Records]] | access-date=2019-05-17}}</ref><ref>{{cite news | first=Haruka | last=Ogasawara | url=https://www.huffingtonpost.jp/entry/story_jp_5c7f3d57e4b06ff26ba374d3 | script-title=ja:リラックマバーガーが可愛すぎる。「ジェラート ピケ カフェ」がコラボした理由は？ | language=ja | work=[[Huffington Post]] | date=2019-03-06 | access-date=2019-05-17 | archive-date=2019-05-17 | archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20190517182438/https://www.huffingtonpost.jp/entry/story_jp_5c7f3d57e4b06ff26ba374d3 | url-status=dead }}</ref>

In September 2017 San-X opened its first temporary [[Pop-up retail|pop-up]] Rilakkuma shop in Florida, US.<ref>{{cite news | first=Ashley | last=Gurbal Kritzer | title=Japanese retailer known for its 'stress-free' bear will open first U.S. store in suburban Tampa | url=https://www.bizjournals.com/tampabay/news/2017/09/26/japanese-retailer-known-for-its-stress-free-bear.html | work=[[Bizjournals]] | date=2017-09-26 | access-date=2019-05-17}}</ref> In September 2018, a second pop-up shop opened at the [[New York City]]'s [[Times Square]].<ref>{{cite web | url=https://www.timessquarenyc.org/locations/rilakkuma-pop-up | title=Rilakkuma Pop-up | work=[[Times Square]] | date=2018-09-14 | url-status=dead | archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20180917034358/https://www.timessquarenyc.org/locations/rilakkuma-pop-up | archive-date=2018-09-17}}</ref>

A [[stop motion]] animated series called ''[[Rilakkuma and Kaoru]]'' began streaming on [[Netflix]] on April 19, 2019. The series was made at Dwarf Studio. It is directed by Masahito Kobayashi and written by [[Naoko Ogigami]], with [[Mikako Tabe]] voicing Kaoru in Japanese and [[Lana Condor]] voicing Kaoru in English.<ref>{{cite web|title=Rilakkuma to Kaoru-san Stop-Motion Anime's Promo, Visual Revealed|url=https://www.animenewsnetwork.com/news/2018-05-22/rilakkuma-to-kaoru-san-stop-motion-anime-promo-visual-revealed/.131872|work=Anime News Network|date=May 22, 2018}}</ref><ref>{{cite web|title=Rilakkuma and Kaoru Stop-Motion Anime Unveils Cast, Trailer, April 19 Debut|url=https://www.animenewsnetwork.com/news/2018-11-26/rilakkuma-and-kaoru-stop-motion-anime-unveils-cast-trailer-april-19-debut/.140004|work=Anime News Network|date=November 26, 2018}}</ref> In October 2020, it was announced another stop-motion series ''Rilakkuma's Theme Park Adventure'' would be released on Netflix.<ref>{{Cite web|url=https://www.animenewsnetwork.com/news/2020-10-26/rilakkuma-returns-with-rilakkuma-theme-park-adventure-series-on-netflix/.165631|title = Rilakkuma Returns with Rilakkuma's Theme Park Adventure Series on Netflix| date=8 June 2023 }}</ref> ''Rilakkuma's Theme Park Adventure'' was released worldwide exclusively on Netflix on August 25, 2022.<ref>{{cite web |url=https://about.netflix.com/en/news/netflix-pavilion-kidzania-fukuoka |title= Anyone Can Be an Animator at Netflix's New Pavilion at KidZania Fukuoka |author= Sakurako Tani |date= 22 July 2022 |page= |publisher= Netflix service |access-date= 15 March 2026}}</ref> It is one of the [[list of highest-grossing media franchises|highest-grossing media franchises]] of all time.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.licenseglobal.com/home/blanc-cerise-inks-deal-rilakkuma |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20200324093858/https://www.licenseglobal.com/home/blanc-cerise-inks-deal-rilakkuma|title=Blanc Cerise Inks Deal with Rilakkuma|website=License Global|archive-date=March 24, 2020|date=March 23, 2020|access-date=January 30, 2021}}</ref>

==Reception==
Initial sales for the Rilakkuma product line were low but slowly picked up popularity in 2004, with Rilakkuma breaking the stereotype of cute characters needing to be energetic.<ref name="japantimes 2019-04-18"/> In May 2010, Rilakkuma ranked as the fifth most popular character in Japan in a survey of the Character Databank.<ref>{{cite news | first=Hiroko | last=Tabuchi | author-link=Hiroko Tabuchi | url=https://www.nytimes.com/2010/05/15/business/global/15kitty.html?src=me&ref=business | title=In Search of Adorable, as Hello Kitty Gets Closer to Goodbye | work=[[The New York Times]] | date=2019-05-14 | access-date=2019-05-17}}</ref> By the end of 2016, Rilakkuma had earned more than 250 billion yen.<ref name="japantimes 2019-04-18" /> {{As of|2020|3}}, character merchandise has generated a total of {{US$|10 billion|long=no}} in retail sales revenue across Asia.<ref>{{cite news |title=Blanc Cerise Inks Deal with Rilakkuma |url=https://www.licenseglobal.com/home/blanc-cerise-inks-deal-rilakkuma |access-date=30 January 2021 |work=License Global |date=23 March 2020}}</ref>
