'''Wanna One''' ({{Korean|워너원}}) was a South Korean [[boy band]] formed by [[CJ E&M]] through the [[Produce 101 (season 2)|second season]] of ''[[Produce 101]]''.<ref>{{cite news|script-title=ko:'프로듀스 101 시즌2' 워너원, 최종 11인 멤버 확정 명단 공개|url=http://star.mbn.co.kr/view.php?&year=2017&no=405937|access-date=June 29, 2017|work=MBN Star|publisher=[[Maeil Broadcasting Network]]|date=June 17, 2017|language=ko|archive-date=June 12, 2018|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20180612141326/http://star.mbn.co.kr/view.php?&year=2017&no=405937|url-status=live}}</ref> The group was composed of eleven members: [[Kang Daniel]], [[Park Ji-hoon]], [[Lee Dae-hwi]], [[Kim Jae-hwan (singer)|Kim Jae-hwan]], [[Ong Seong-wu]], [[Park Woo-jin]], [[Lai Kuan-lin]], [[Yoon Ji-sung]], [[Hwang Min-hyun]], [[Bae Jin-young]], and [[Ha Sung-woon]]. The group debuted on August 7, 2017, under [[Swing Entertainment]] and [[CJ E&M Music Performance Division|CJ E&M]]. Their contract ended on December 31, 2018, but their final activity as a group was their single album B-side released on January 27, 2022.

==History==
===Pre-debut: ''Produce 101''===
{{Main|Produce 101 (season 2)}}
Wanna One was formed through the 'survival' competition series [[Produce 101 (season 2)|''Produce 101'' (season 2)]], which aired on [[Mnet (TV channel)|Mnet]] from April 7 until June 16, 2017. Out of an initial [[List of Produce 101 contestants (season 2)|101 trainees]] representing various agencies, the final 11 were chosen by audience voting and announced via live television broadcast.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.koreajoongangdaily.com/korea/produce-101-picks-wanna-one-performers/11488010|title='Produce 101' picks Wanna One performers|date=June 19, 2017|work=[[Korea JoongAng Daily]]|access-date=August 10, 2017|archive-date=March 27, 2019|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20190327090212/http://koreajoongangdaily.joins.com/news/article/Article.aspx?aid=3034767|url-status=live}}</ref>

Before appearing on the program, several members had already been active in the entertainment industry. [[Park Ji-hoon]] was a [[child actor]] and had appeared in [[Korean drama|drama series]] such as ''[[Kimchi Cheese Smile]]'' and ''[[The King and I (TV series)|The King & I]]''.<ref name="munhwa 20170418">{{cite news|last=Lee|first=Na-kyung|script-title=ko:"박지훈 잘 컸네" '프로듀스101 1위 박지훈, 10년 전 사극서 '거세 연기'로 주목받은 과거 화제|url=http://www.munhwanews.com/news/articleView.html?idxno=49714|access-date=June 27, 2017|work=[[Munhwa Ilbo]]|date=April 18, 2017|language=ko|archive-date=June 16, 2018|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20180616103958/http://www.munhwanews.com/news/articleView.html?idxno=49714|url-status=live}}</ref> He also made appearances on several variety shows alongside the boy bands [[Big Bang (South Korean band)|Big Bang]] and [[SS501]].<ref name="pjh pdb">{{cite web|script-title=ko:박지훈|url=http://www.playdb.co.kr/artistdb/detail.asp?ManNo=19515|website=PlayDB|publisher=Interpark|access-date=June 17, 2017|archive-date=June 12, 2019|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20190612172118/http://www.playdb.co.kr/artistdb/detail.asp?ManNo=19515|url-status=live}}</ref> In 2012, [[Hwang Min-hyun]] made his musical debut as a member of the group [[NU'EST]]. [[Kim Jae-hwan (singer)|Kim Jae-hwan]] made his first appearance on [[Korea's Got Talent (season 2)|season 2 of ''Korea's Got Talent'']] but was eliminated during the semi-final. In 2014, [[Ha Sung-woon]] had previously debuted as a member of the group [[Hotshot (band)|Hotshot]].

=== 2019: Final concert and disbandment ===
On December 18, 2018, Swing Entertainment released an official statement that the group's contract will end on its original planned date, December 31, 2018.<ref name= "Newsen 2018-12-18">{{Cite news|url=https://m.entertain.naver.com/now/read?oid=609&aid=0000032980|title=워너원 측 "12월31일 계약종료, 1월 콘서트로 마무리"(공식입장 전문)|access-date=2018-12-18|language=ko|archive-date=2019-04-02|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20190402081026/https://m.entertain.naver.com/now/read?oid=609&aid=0000032980|url-status=live}}</ref> Swing also stated that all agencies came to an agreement that will allow the members to attend scheduled year-end shows and award ceremonies throughout January 2019.<ref name= "Newsen 2018-12-18" /> The group concluded activities with a final concert (titled ''Therefore 2019'')''.<ref name="Newsen 2018-12-18" />'' The concert was held at the Gocheok Sky Dome in Seoul, where the group held their debut showcase.
