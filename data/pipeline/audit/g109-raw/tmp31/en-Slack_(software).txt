'''Slack''' is a [[Cloud computing|cloud-based]] team communication platform developed by [[Slack Technologies]], which has been owned by [[Salesforce]] since 2021.<ref>{{Cite web |last=Slack |title=About us |url=https://slack.com/intl/en-gb/about |access-date=2026-03-31 |website=Slack |language=en-IN}}</ref> Slack uses a [[freemium|freemium model]]. Slack is primarily offered as a [[business-to-business]] service, with its user base being predominantly team-based businesses while its features are focused on business administration and communication.<ref>{{Cite web |last=Slack |title=What is Slack? |url=https://slack.com/help/articles/115004071768-What-is-Slack- |access-date=2024-04-01 |website=Slack Help Center |language=en-US}}</ref><ref name=":7">{{Cite web |last=John |first=Steven |title='What is Slack?' Everything you need to know about the professional messaging program. |url=https://www.businessinsider.com/what-is-slack |access-date=2022-05-04 |website=Business Insider |language=en-US}}</ref>

==History==
Slack originated as an internal communication tool used within [[Stewart Butterfield]]'s company [[Tiny Speck]], during its work on the development of ''[[Glitch (video game)|Glitch]]'', an online video game.<ref name="Tam">{{cite web |last=Tam |first=Donna |title=Flickr founder plans to kill company e-mails with Slack |url=https://www.cnet.com/news/flickr-founder-plans-to-kill-company-e-mails-with-slack/ |url-status=live |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20131203003818/http://news.cnet.com/8301-1001_3-57598469-92/flickr-founder-plans-to-kill-company-e-mails-with-slack/ |archive-date=December 3, 2013 |access-date=October 13, 2018}}</ref> These communication tools were initially built around the [[Internet Relay Chat]] (IRC) protocol and included scripts designed to automate and organize file exchanges among its development team. By October 2012, Stewart Butterfield realized that ''Glitch'' was not going to be profitable.<ref>{{Cite magazine |last=Honan |first=Mat |title=How Stewart Butterfield Created Slack From a Failed Video Game |language=en-US |magazine=Wired |url=https://www.wired.com/2014/08/the-most-fascinating-profile-youll-ever-read-about-a-guy-and-his-boring-startup/ |access-date=2023-08-30 |issn=1059-1028}}</ref> As a result, he decided to change the direction of his company and repurpose the communication tools they had developed into a new product.

In 2012, Butterfield said the name "Slack" was derived from the phrase "Searchable Log of All Conversation and Knowledge", replacing the previous codename "linefeed".<ref name="Acronym">{{Cite news |url=https://www.businessinsider.com/where-did-slack-get-its-name-2016-9 |title=Slack, the red hot $3.8 billion startup, has a hidden meaning behind its name |last=Kim |first=Eugene |date=September 27, 2016 |work=UK Business Insider |access-date=October 13, 2018 |archive-date=November 19, 2019 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20191119051728/https://www.businessinsider.com/where-did-slack-get-its-name-2016-9 |url-status=live }}</ref>

In August 2013, Slack was launched to the public<ref name="Koetsier">{{cite web |last=Koetsier |first=John |date=August 15, 2013 |title=Flickr founder Stewart Butterfield's new Slack signed up 8,000 companies in 24 hours |url=https://venturebeat.com/business/flickr-founder-stewart-butterfields-new-slack-signed-up-8000-companies-in-24-hours |url-status=live |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20200204164223/https://venturebeat.com/2013/08/15/flickr-founder-stewart-butterfields-new-slack-signed-up-8000-companies-in-24-hours/ |archive-date=February 4, 2020 |access-date=October 13, 2018 |work=VentureBeat}}</ref> and continued to maintain compatibility with [[IRC]], reflecting its origin.<ref>{{Cite web |last=Mombrea |first=Matthew |date=2014-08-15 |title=Slack: I've seen the future, and it reminds me of the past |url=https://www.computerworld.com/article/2833495/slack--i-ve-seen-the-future--and-it-reminds-me-of-the-past.html |access-date=2023-08-31 |website=Computerworld |language=en}}</ref>  Additionally, it was compatible with [[XMPP]] messaging protocols. In May 2018 however, the company chose to close down these corresponding gateways due to limitations inherent in those protocols.<ref>{{Cite news |url=https://www.theregister.com/2018/03/09/slack_cuts_ties_to_irc_and_xmpp/ |title=Slack cuts ties to IRC and XMPP, cos they don't speak Emoji |date=March 9, 2018 |last=Sharwood |first=Simon |access-date=October 13, 2018 |archive-date=September 3, 2018 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20180903035033/https://www.theregister.co.uk/2018/03/09/slack_cuts_ties_to_irc_and_xmpp/ |url-status=live }}</ref>

On July 26, 2018, Slack acquired [[HipChat]] and [[Stride (software)|Stride]] from [[Atlassian]], with plans to shut down both services in February 2019 and migrate their users to Slack.<ref>{{Cite news |last=Etienne |first=Stefan |date=July 26, 2018 |title=Slack buys HipChat with plans to shut it down and migrate users to its chat service |work=The Verge |url=https://www.theverge.com/2018/7/26/17619482/slack-hipchat-acquisition-stride-atlassian-partnership-microsoft-teams-competition |url-status=live |access-date=October 13, 2018 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20180814072138/https://www.theverge.com/2018/7/26/17619482/slack-hipchat-acquisition-stride-atlassian-partnership-microsoft-teams-competition |archive-date=August 14, 2018}}</ref><ref>{{Cite news |last=Kumparak |first=Greg |date=July 26, 2018 |title=Atlassian's HipChat and Stride to be discontinued, with Slack buying up the IP |work=[[TechCrunch]] |url=https://techcrunch.com/2018/07/26/atlassians-hipchat-and-stride-to-be-discontinued-with-slack-buying-up-the-ip/ |url-status=live |access-date=October 13, 2018 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20181009041300/https://techcrunch.com/2018/07/26/atlassians-hipchat-and-stride-to-be-discontinued-with-slack-buying-up-the-ip/ |archive-date=October 9, 2018}}</ref>

In June 2019, Slack went public through a [[direct public offering]] to reach a market value of $19.5&nbsp;billion.<ref>{{cite web|archive-date=March 15, 2021 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20210315170510/https://www.nytimes.com/2019/06/20/technology/slack-stock-ipo-price-trading.html |author=Erin Griffith |date=20 June 2019 |title=Slack Stock Soars, Putting Company's Public Value at $19.5 Billion |url=https://www.nytimes.com/2019/06/20/technology/slack-stock-ipo-price-trading.html |website=The New York Times}}</ref>

In July 2020, Slack acquired Rimeto, a startup specializing in employee directories and profiles,<ref>{{cite news |last1=Konrad |first1=Alex |date=July 8, 2020 |title=Slack Acquires Corporate Directory Startup Rimeto, Plans To Operate It As Standalone App |work=Forbes |url=https://www.forbes.com/sites/alexkonrad/2020/07/08/slack-acquires-corporate-directory-startup-rimeto/#3e1a76c040d0 |url-status=live |access-date=9 July 2020 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20200709020157/https://www.forbes.com/sites/alexkonrad/2020/07/08/slack-acquires-corporate-directory-startup-rimeto/#3e1a76c040d0 |archive-date=July 9, 2020}}</ref><ref>{{cite news |last1=Miller |first1=Ron |date=July 8, 2020 |title=Slack snags corporate directory startup Rimeto to up its people search game |publisher=TechCrunch |url=https://techcrunch.com/2020/07/08/slack-snags-corporate-directory-startup-rimeto-to-up-its-people-search-game/ |url-status=live |access-date=9 July 2020 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20200709020157/https://techcrunch.com/2020/07/08/slack-snags-corporate-directory-startup-rimeto-to-up-its-people-search-game/ |archive-date=July 9, 2020}}</ref> and in June 2021, its functionality was launched as an optional feature within Slack named "Slack Atlas".<ref>{{Cite web |last=Miller |first=Ron |date=2021-06-30 |title=Slack's new voice, video tools should fit nicely on Salesforce platform after deal closes |url=https://techcrunch.com/2021/06/30/slacks-new-voice-video-tools-should-fit-nicely-on-salesforce-platform-after-deal-closes/ |access-date=2023-09-08 |website=TechCrunch |language=en-US}}</ref>

On December 1, 2020, Slack and [[Salesforce]] announced an agreement for Salesforce to acquire the company for approximately $27.7&nbsp;billion.<ref name="salesforce">{{cite news |url=https://investor.salesforce.com/press-releases/press-release-details/2020/Salesforce-Signs-Definitive-Agreement-to-Acquire-Slack/default.aspx |title=Salesforce Signs Definitive Agreement to Acquire Slack |access-date=January 21, 2021 |archive-date=January 21, 2021 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20210121200732/https://investor.salesforce.com/press-releases/press-release-details/2020/Salesforce-Signs-Definitive-Agreement-to-Acquire-Slack/default.aspx |url-status=live }}</ref><ref>{{Cite web |last=Levy |first=Ari |date=2020-12-01 |title=Salesforce acquires Slack for over $27 billion, marking cloud software vendor's largest deal ever |url=https://www.cnbc.com/2020/12/01/salesforce-buys-slack-for-27point7-billion-in-cloud-companys-largest-deal.html |access-date=2023-09-08 |website=CNBC |language=en}}</ref> The acquisition was completed on July 21, 2021.<ref>{{cite web |title=Salesforce closes $27.7 billion acquisition of Slack |url=https://finance.yahoo.com/news/salesforce-completes-slack-acquisition-132247106.html |access-date=2021-07-21 |website=finance.yahoo.com |date=July 21, 2021 |language=en-US}}</ref>

On December 5, 2022, Salesforce announced that Butterfield was leaving Slack and would be succeeded by [[Lidiane Jones]], an executive vice president at Salesforce.<ref>{{cite web |title=Slack CEO Stewart Butterfield is leaving Salesforce two years after a deal was announced |url=https://www.cnbc.com/2022/12/05/slack-ceo-stewart-butterfield-leaving-salesforce-two-years-after-deal.html |access-date=2022-12-05 |website=CNBC |date=December 5, 2022 |language=en-US}}</ref> On November 13, 2023, Salesforce executive Denise Dresser was appointed to replace Jones.<ref>{{Cite web |last=Goswami |first=Rohan |date=2023-11-13 |title=Salesforce appoints longtime executive Denise Dresser as Slack CEO |url=https://www.cnbc.com/2023/11/13/saleforce-appoints-longtime-executive-denise-dresser-as-slack-ceo.html |access-date=2023-11-14 |website=CNBC |language=en}}</ref><ref>{{cite web | url=https://techcrunch.com/2023/11/13/salesforce-names-denise-dresser-to-succeed-lidiane-jones-as-ceo-of-slack/ | title=Salesforce names Denise Dresser to succeed Lidiane Jones as CEO of Slack | date=November 13, 2023 }}</ref> Dresser departed for [[OpenAI]] in December 2025, and Rob Seaman was named interim CEO.<ref>{{Cite news |last=Schiffer |first=Zoë |title=OpenAI Hires Slack CEO as New Chief Revenue Officer |url=https://www.wired.com/story/slack-ceo-denise-dresser-joins-openai-chief-revenue-officer/ |access-date=2026-03-08 |work=Wired |language=en-US |issn=1059-1028}}</ref>

In August 2024, [[Box, Inc.|Box]] and Slack announced an expanded partnership that introduced secure AI capabilities to enterprise content management.<ref>{{Cite web |last=Sophy |first=Joshua |date=2024-08-15 |title=Box and Slack Expand Partnership |url=https://smallbiztrends.com/box-ai-slack-partnership-expands/ |access-date=2024-08-19 |website=Small Business Trends |language=en-us}}</ref><ref>{{Cite web |last=MacRae |first=Duncan |date=2024-08-19 |title=Box and Slack expand partnership with the launch of Box AI in Slack |url=https://www.marketingtechnews.net/news/2024/aug/19/box-and-slack-expand-partnership-with-the-launch-of-box-ai-in-slack/ |access-date=2024-08-19 |website=Marketing Tech News |language=en-GB}}</ref>

===<span class="anchor" id="Security Incidents"></span> Outages and security breaches===
In March 2015, Slack announced that it had been [[Exploit (computer security)|hacked]] for over four days in February 2015 and that some data associated with user accounts had been compromised, including email addresses, usernames, hashed passwords, phone numbers, and [[Skype]] IDs. In response to the attacks, Slack added [[Multi-factor authentication|two-factor authentication]] to its service.<ref>{{Cite magazine |last1=Greenberg |first1=Andy |title=Slack Says It Was Hacked, Enables Two-Factor Authentication |url=https://www.wired.com/2015/03/slack-admits-hacked-enables-2-factor-authentication/ |magazine=Wired |publisher=Condé Nast |access-date=October 13, 2018 |date=March 27, 2015 |archive-date=February 4, 2020 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20200204164224/https://www.wired.com/2015/03/slack-admits-hacked-enables-2-factor-authentication/ |url-status=live }}</ref>

On January 4, 2021, Slack suffered a significant outage that lasted several hours. From 10&nbsp;AM ET until 3&nbsp;PM ET, users could not log in, send or receive messages, place or answer calls, or use Slack connections. After 3&nbsp;PM, most of the core features became operational, except for push notifications, email, and third-party integrations, including [[Google Calendar]] and [[Outlook.com|Outlook Calendar]].<ref>{{cite web |last=Lyons |first=Kim |date=2021-01-04 |title=Slack starts 2021 with a massive outage |url=https://www.theverge.com/2021/1/4/22213105/slack-outage-down-2021-server-error |access-date=2021-01-05 |website=The Verge |language=en |archive-date=January 5, 2021 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20210105141521/https://www.theverge.com/2021/1/4/22213105/slack-outage-down-2021-server-error |url-status=live}}</ref><ref>{{cite web |date=2021-01-04 |title=Slack kicks off 2021 with a global outage |url=https://apnews.com/article/slack-global-outage-technology-592c53b7ef26fe526f7e397138b86493 |access-date=2021-01-04 |website=AP NEWS |archive-date=January 4, 2021 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20210104174953/https://apnews.com/article/slack-global-outage-technology-592c53b7ef26fe526f7e397138b86493 |url-status=live}}</ref>

In 2022, Slack suffered widely reported outages on February 22,<ref>{{cite web | url=https://www.theregister.com/2022/02/22/slack_down/ | title=Ongoing services outage at Slack }}</ref> March 9<ref>{{cite web | url=https://www.theverge.com/2022/3/9/22968785/slack-down-outage-messages-edit-chat-connection-problem | title=Slack says its problems are solved, but you might have to refresh | date=March 9, 2022 }}</ref> and July 26.<ref>{{cite web | url=https://www.ctvnews.ca/sci-tech/article/slack-back-online-after-users-report-outage-affecting-sending-messages/ | title=Slack back online after users report outage affecting sending messages | date=July 26, 2022 }}</ref> On December 31, 2022, Slack announced that its private [[GitHub]] repositories had been compromised during the previous weeks, using stolen security tokens.<ref name="2023-01-05_BC">{{cite web |url=https://www.bleepingcomputer.com/news/security/slacks-private-github-code-repositories-stolen-over-holidays/ |title=Slack's private GitHub code repositories stolen over holidays |author=Ax Sharma |website=[[Bleeping Computer]] |date=2023-01-05}}</ref>

In February 2025, Slack experienced a widely reported outage.<ref>{{Cite web |date=2025-02-26 |title=Slack down: Users report issues with app and website |url=https://www.independent.co.uk/news/world/americas/slack-down-detector-outage-status-b2705412.html |access-date=2025-05-12 |website=The Independent |language=en}}</ref> In June, in response to increasing scrutiny about AI firms and their handling of personal and customer data,<ref>{{Cite web |date=2025-06-11 |title=Salesforce blocks AI rivals from using Slack data, The Information reports |url=https://www.reuters.com/business/salesforce-blocks-ai-rivals-using-slack-data-information-reports-2025-06-11/ |access-date=2026-04-03 |website=[[Reuters]]}}</ref> Slack began blocking LLMs from indexing, copying, or storing data accessed via the Slack API on a long-term basis.<ref>{{Cite web |last=Dunn |first=John |date=2025-06-11 |title=Salesforce changes Slack API terms to block bulk data access for LLMs |url=https://www.computerworld.com/article/4005509/salesforce-changes-slack-api-terms-to-block-bulk-data-access-for-llms.html |access-date=2026-04-03 |website=Computerworld |language=English}}</ref>

==Features==
Slack offers many [[Internet Relay Chat|IRC]]-style features, including persistent chat rooms known as "channels", which are organized by topic, as well as private groups and direct-messaging functionalities.<ref>{{Cite journal |last=Johnson |first=Heather A. |date=January 2018 |title=Slack |journal=Journal of the Medical Library Association |volume=106 |issue=1 |pages=148–151 |doi=10.5195/jmla.2018.315 |issn=1536-5050 |pmc=5764588}}</ref> All content, including files, conversations, and people, is searchable within Slack. Users can express their reactions in the form of [[Emoji|emojis]] to any message.<ref>{{cite web |last=Crook |first=Jordan |date=July 9, 2015 |title=Slack Adds Emoji Reactions |url=https://techcrunch.com/2015/07/09/slack-adds-emoji-reactions/ |url-status=live |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20181130213252/https://techcrunch.com/2015/07/09/slack-adds-emoji-reactions/ |archive-date=November 30, 2018 |access-date=October 13, 2018}}</ref> 

Slack allows communities, groups, or teams to join a "workspace" via a specific [[URL]] or invitation sent by a team admin or owner.<ref>{{Cite web |last=Krasnoff |first=Barbara |date=2022-01-28 |title=How to set up a Slack account |url=https://www.theverge.com/22905189/slack-account-set-up-messages-channels-work-remote-office |access-date=2023-09-08 |website=The Verge |language=en-US}}</ref> A workspace can contain both public and private channels, with public channels being accessible to all members of the workspace.<ref name=":3">{{cite web |last=John |first=Steven |title=How to join a Slack channel on desktop or mobile, whether it's public or private |url=https://www.businessinsider.com/how-to-join-a-slack-channel |url-status=live |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20200609192244/https://www.businessinsider.com/how-to-join-a-slack-channel |archive-date=June 9, 2020 |access-date=2020-06-09 |website=Business Insider}}</ref> Both public and private channels can be converted interchangeably.<ref>{{Cite web |last=Slack |title=Convert a channel to private |url=https://slack.com/help/articles/213185467-Convert-a-channel-to-private-or-public#convert-to-private-1 |access-date=2023-07-28 |website=Slack Help Center |language=en-US}}</ref>

Slack allows users to incorporate and customize [[chatbot]]s known as "Slackbots". These chatbots can be configured to send notifications, reminders, or provide tailored responses to specific phrases, among other functions.<ref>{{Cite web |last=Newlands |first=Murray |date=2017-09-11 |title=How to Make A 'Slackbot' for Free Without Coding |url=https://www.entrepreneur.com/science-technology/how-to-make-a-slackbot-for-free-without-coding/300023 |access-date=2022-09-14 |website=Entrepreneur |language=en}}</ref> Furthermore, Slack provides an [[API|application programming interface]] that enables users to develop applications and automate various processes, including sending notifications based on input, generating alerts for specific conditions, creating internal support tickets, and more.<ref>{{cite web |title=Slack API |url=https://api.slack.com/ |url-status=live |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20181228041601/https://api.slack.com/ |archive-date=December 28, 2018 |access-date=28 December 2018 |website=Slack}}</ref> In January 2026, Salesforce transitioned Slackbot from an automated assistant into an integrated [[agentic AI]], using [[Anthropic]]'s [[Claude (language model)|Claude]] model.<ref>{{Cite web |last=Bonk |first=Lawrence |date=2025-10-13 |title=Slack is transforming its Slackbot into a 'personalized AI companion' |url=https://www.engadget.com/ai/slack-is-transforming-its-slackbot-into-a-personalized-ai-companion-154156367.html |access-date=2026-03-26 |website=Engadget |language=en-US}}</ref><ref>{{Cite web |last=Novet |first=Jordan |date=2026-01-13 |title=Salesforce releases updated Slackbot powered by Anthropic's AI model |url=https://www.cnbc.com/2026/01/13/salesforce-releases-updated-slackbot-powered-by-anthropics-ai-model.html |access-date=2026-03-26 |website=CNBC |language=en}}</ref>

In March 2022, Slack introduced a new [[Telephone call|voice conference]] feature known as "huddles".<ref>{{Cite web |last=Slack |title=New collaboration tools to help you work from anywhere |url=https://slack.com/intl/en-gb/blog/collaboration/new-tools-to-help-you-work-from-anywhere |access-date=2022-06-08 |website=Slack |language=en-GB}}</ref> Within a Huddle, users have the capability to mute or unmute themselves, [[Screen sharing|share their screens]], draw on a shared screen, and invite others to the call.<ref>{{Cite web |title=How to use Slack's huddle feature (and why you should) |url=https://www.zdnet.com/home-and-office/work-life/how-to-use-slacks-huddle-feature-and-why-you-should/ |access-date=2023-09-08 |website=ZDNET |language=en}}</ref> In June 2022, Huddles received the additional functionality of video calls.<ref>{{Cite web |last=Pierce |first=David |date=2022-06-22 |title=Slack added video to Huddles so you don't have to spend your life in Zoom meetings |url=https://www.theverge.com/2022/6/22/23177239/slack-huddles-video-audio-chat-call |access-date=2023-09-08 |website=The Verge |language=en-US}}</ref> Huddles are restricted to only two participants on free tiers, while paid plans offer the capacity for up to 50 participants.

In March 2023, Salesforce announced the partnership with [[OpenAI]] to launch a [[ChatGPT]] integration for Slack that can be used for summarizing conversations, answering questions, or drafting replies.<ref>{{Cite web |last=Roth |first=Emma |date=2023-03-07 |title=Slack's new ChatGPT bot will talk to your colleagues for you |url=https://www.theverge.com/2023/3/7/23628673/chatgpt-slack-salesforce-einstein-ai-business-messaging |access-date=2023-09-08 |website=The Verge |language=en-US}}</ref> In May 2025, Salesforce added [[Agentforce]] to Slack, allowing companies to build and deploy task-specific AI agents.<ref>{{Cite news |date=2025-05-19 |title=Salesforce just unveiled AI 'digital teammates' in Slack — and they're coming for Microsoft Copilot |url=https://venturebeat.com/ai/salesforce-just-unveiled-ai-digital-teammates-in-slack-and-theyre-coming-for-microsoft-copilot |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20260116021018/https://venturebeat.com/ai/salesforce-just-unveiled-ai-digital-teammates-in-slack-and-theyre-coming-for-microsoft-copilot |archive-date=January 16, 2026 |access-date=2026-03-21 |work=Venturebeat |language=en-US |url-status=live }}</ref>

In October 2025 at Dreamforce, the Agentforce 360 platform was announced, with Slack as the frontend "agentic OS".<ref>{{Cite web |last=Devlin |first=Kieran |date=2025-10-21 |title=Is Slack's 'Agentic OS' Reinvention the Future of Work Platforms? |url=https://www.uctoday.com/unified-communications/slack-agentic-os-dreamforce-2025/ |access-date=2026-04-20 |website=UC Today |language=en-GB}}</ref> [[Anthropic]] launched [[Claude (language model)#Claude Code|Claude Code]] as a beta feature within Slack in December 2025, allowing developers to code tasks directly from chat threads.<ref>{{Cite web |last=Bellan |first=Rebecca |date=2025-12-08 |title=Claude Code is coming to Slack, and that's a bigger deal than it sounds |url=https://techcrunch.com/2025/12/08/claude-code-is-coming-to-slack-and-thats-a-bigger-deal-than-it-sounds/ |access-date=2026-04-15 |website=TechCrunch |language=en-US}}</ref>

Slack provides mobile apps for [[iOS]] and [[Android (operating system)|Android]] in addition to their web browser client and desktop clients for [[macOS]], [[Microsoft Windows|Windows]],<ref>{{cite web |first1=Mary Jo |last1=Foley |access-date=2020-04-30 |title=How Slack brought its desktop app to the Windows Store |url=https://www.zdnet.com/article/how-slack-brought-its-desktop-app-to-the-windows-store/ |website=ZDNet |archive-date=August 7, 2020 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20200807073939/https://www.zdnet.com/article/how-slack-brought-its-desktop-app-to-the-windows-store/ |url-status=live}}</ref> and [[Linux]] (beta).<ref>{{Cite web |last=Warren |first=Tom |date=2019-09-12 |title=Slack launches dark mode for macOS, Windows, and Linux |url=https://www.theverge.com/2019/9/12/20862234/slack-dark-mode-macos-windows-linux-desktop-features |access-date=2022-12-02 |website=The Verge |language=en-US}}</ref><ref>{{cite web |title=System requirements for using Slack |url=https://slack.com/help/articles/115002037526-System-requirements-for-using-Slack |website=Slack Help Center}}</ref> Slack is also available for the [[Apple Watch]], allowing users to send direct messages, see mentions, and make simple replies.<ref>{{cite web |title=Slack brings its app to the Apple Watch (video) |url=https://venturebeat.com/enterprise/slack-brings-its-app-to-the-apple-watch-video |work=VentureBeat |date=April 20, 2015 |first=Jordan |last=Novet |access-date=October 13, 2018 |archive-date=June 26, 2018 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20180626164441/https://venturebeat.com/2015/04/20/slack-brings-its-app-to-the-apple-watch-video/ |url-status=live }}</ref>

==Business model==
Slack is a [[freemium]] product whose main paid features, limited to Pro and Business plans,<ref>{{Cite web |last=Brandon |first=John |date=2025-08-26 |title=Slack Review: The Top Team Communication App for the Workplace |url=https://www.pcmag.com/reviews/slack |access-date=2026-03-15 |website=PCMAG |language=en}}</ref> are the ability to search more than 90 days' archived messages and add unlimited apps and integrations.<ref name="paid vs free">{{Cite web |title=Slack paid vs. free {{!}} Slack |url=https://slack.com/pricing/paid-vs-free#:~:text=Unlimited%20messaging-,90-day%20history,-Unlimited%5B%5D |access-date=2024-08-14 |website=slack.com}}</ref> They also claim support for an unlimited number of users. When [[freeCodeCamp]] attempted to switch its community of over 8,000 users to Slack in 2015, however, they experienced many technical issues and were advised by Slack support to limit their channels to "no more than 1,000 users (ideally more like 500)".<ref>{{cite web |url=https://medium.freecodecamp.org/so-yeah-we-tried-slack-and-we-deeply-regretted-it-391bcc714c81 |title=So Yeah We Tried Slack... and We Deeply Regretted It |publisher=freeCodeCamp |date=June 21, 2015 |access-date=October 13, 2018 |archive-date=October 25, 2018 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20181025180132/https://medium.freecodecamp.org/so-yeah-we-tried-slack-and-we-deeply-regretted-it-391bcc714c81 |url-status=live }}</ref><ref>{{cite web |url=https://www.businessinsider.com/slack-free-unlimited-plan-has-limits-2015-6 |title=Startup founder claims $2.8 billion startup Slack is misleading people about its free 'unlimited' plan |first=Eugene |last=Kim |publisher=BusinessInsider.com |date=June 22, 2015 |access-date=October 13, 2018 |archive-date=November 2, 2018 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20181102052000/https://www.businessinsider.com/slack-free-unlimited-plan-has-limits-2015-6 |url-status=live }}</ref> That specific limit no longer applied by January 2017.<ref>{{cite web |url=https://davechen.net/2017/01/slack-user-limit/ |title=Despite its marketing, Slack's free tier limits your total number of users |publisher=DaveChen.net |first=Dave |last=Chen |date=January 20, 2017 |access-date=October 13, 2018 |archive-date=July 20, 2018 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20180720051658/https://davechen.net/2017/01/slack-user-limit/ |url-status=live }}</ref>

==Reception==
In March 2015, the ''[[Financial Times]]'' wrote that Slack was the first business technology to have crossed from business into personal use since [[Microsoft Office]] and the [[BlackBerry]].<ref name=":6">{{Cite news |url=https://www.ft.com/content/bd7dbf46-d24c-11e4-9c25-00144feab7de |title=Slack: workplace message app so cute you want to use it at home |newspaper=Financial Times |date=March 25, 2015 |access-date=October 13, 2018 |archive-date=October 14, 2018 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20181014053036/https://www.ft.com/content/bd7dbf46-d24c-11e4-9c25-00144feab7de |url-status=live }}{{Subscription required}}</ref> In 2017, [[New York (magazine)|''New York'']] magazine criticized the platform for being "another utility we both rely on and resent".<ref>{{Cite news |first=Molly |last=Fischer |url=https://nymag.com/intelligencer/2017/05/what-has-slack-done-to-the-office.html |title=What Happens When Work Becomes a Nonstop Chat Room |newspaper=New York Magazine |date=May 17, 2017 |access-date=October 13, 2018 |archive-date=October 20, 2018 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20181020225446/http://nymag.com/intelligencer/2017/05/what-has-slack-done-to-the-office.html |url-status=live }}</ref>

In 2017, Slack was recognized as the best startup of the year at the [[Crunchies]] Awards, organized by [[TechCrunch]].<ref>{{cite news |url=https://techcrunch.com/2017/02/06/and-the-winners-of-the-10th-annual-crunchies-are/ |title=And the winners of the 10th Annual Crunchies are... |first=Frederic |last=Lardinois |date=February 6, 2017 |work=[[TechCrunch]] |access-date=October 13, 2018 |archive-date=February 22, 2017 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20170222194307/https://techcrunch.com/2017/02/06/and-the-winners-of-the-10th-annual-crunchies-are/ |url-status=live }}</ref>

The digital rights group [[Electronic Frontier Foundation]] (EFF) has cautioned that "Slack stores and is able to read all of your communications, as well as identifying information for everyone in your workspace."<ref name=":0">{{Cite news |url=https://www.eff.org/deeplinks/2018/02/revolution-and-slack |title=The Revolution and Slack |last1=Cohn |first1=Cindy |date=February 14, 2018 |work=Electronic Frontier Foundation |access-date=October 13, 2018 |last2=Gebhart |first2=Gennie |author-link=Cindy Cohn |archive-date=September 4, 2018 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20180904114531/https://www.eff.org/deeplinks/2018/02/revolution-and-slack |url-status=live }}</ref> They commended the company for following several best practices in standing up for users concerning government data requests, such as requiring a warrant for content stored on its server, and awarding it four out of five stars in its 2017 "Who has your back" report;<ref>{{Cite news |url=https://www.eff.org/who-has-your-back-2017 |title=Who Has Your Back? Government Data Requests 2017 |date=July 10, 2017 |first=Rainey |last=Reitman |work=Electronic Frontier Foundation |access-date=October 13, 2018 |archive-date=September 15, 2018 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20180915003333/https://www.eff.org/who-has-your-back-2017 |url-status=live }}</ref> the EFF also criticized Slack for "a broad set of exceptions" to its promise to notify users of such requests, and for other privacy shortcomings.<ref name=":0" />

servers under Slack control.<ref name="slack-alternatives">{{Cite magazine |url=https://www.wired.com/2016/03/open-source-devs-racing-build-better-versions-slack/ |title=Open sourcers race to build
