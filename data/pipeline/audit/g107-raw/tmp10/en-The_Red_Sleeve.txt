'''''The Red Sleeve''''' ({{Korean|옷소매 붉은 끝동}}) is a [[South Korean television series]] starring [[Lee Jun-ho (entertainer)|Lee Jun-ho]], [[Lee Se-young]], and [[Kang Hoon]].<ref name="Genre" /><ref name="Company" /> It is a fictionalised account of the lives of [[Jeongjo of Joseon|King Jeongjo]] and [[Royal Noble Consort Uibin Seong|Consort Ui]]. It aired on [[MBC TV (South Korean TV channel)|MBC TV]] from November 12, 2021, to January 1, 2022, every Friday and Saturday at 22:00 ([[Time in South Korea|KST]]).<ref>{{cite web|title=MBC 금토극 신설...첫 주자 '검은태양' 9월 방송 (공식)|trans-title=New MBC Friday-Saturday drama...First runner 'Black Sun' broadcast in September (official)|url=https://entertain.naver.com/read?oid=052&aid=0001617524|via=[[Naver]]|publisher=[[YTN]]|language=ko|last=Oh|first=Ji-won|date=July 22, 2021|access-date=November 20, 2021|archive-date=October 20, 2021|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20211020022449/https://entertain.naver.com/read?oid=052&aid=0001617524|url-status=live}}</ref>

==Synopsis==
Based on a novel of the same name, ''The Red Sleeve'' tells the story of a royal court romance between the king of [[Joseon]] who places his duty to his country above love, and a [[Kungnyŏ|court lady]] who wants to protect the life she has chosen.<ref name="Genre" /> The story is told from the viewpoint of the court lady.
