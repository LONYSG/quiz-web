'''''Mr. Queen''''' ({{Korean|철인왕후}}) is a 2020 [[South Korean television series]] based on the Chinese web series ''[[Go Princess Go]]''. Directed by [[Yoon Sung-sik]], it stars [[Shin Hye-sun]] as [[Queen Cheorin]] and [[Kim Jung-hyun (actor, born 1990)|Kim Jung-hyun]] as [[Cheoljong of Joseon|King Cheoljong]]. The drama is about Queen Cheorin finding herself with the soul of a modern-day man inside her body and King Cheoljong striving to be a king who helps his people.<ref>{{Cite web|title=Queen Cheorin What Are The Expected Release Date? And What You Should Know About The Show?|work=Pop Culture Times |url=https://popculturetimes.com/2020/07/25/queen-cheorin-what-are-the-expected-release-date-and-what-you-should-know-about-the-show/|date=July 25, 2020|access-date=October 20, 2020|archive-date=October 23, 2020|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20201023154938/https://popculturetimes.com/2020/07/25/queen-cheorin-what-are-the-expected-release-date-and-what-you-should-know-about-the-show/|url-status=live}}</ref><ref>{{Cite web|title=Queen Cheorin (Korean Drama - 2020) - 철인왕후|url=https://www.hancinema.net/korean_drama_Queen_Cheorin.php|website=[[HanCinema]]|access-date=October 20, 2020|archive-date=October 9, 2020|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20201009130100/https://www.hancinema.net/korean_drama_Queen_Cheorin.php|url-status=live}}</ref> It aired on [[TVN (South Korean TV channel)|tvN]] every Saturday and Sunday at 21:00 ([[Time in South Korea|KST]]) from December 12, 2020, to February 14, 2021.<ref>{{cite web|title=Mr. Queen (2020)|url=https://watch.cjenm.com/broadcast/sub/view?topcategory=DRM10&formatYn=N&contentsId=B120209759|website=CJ Entertainment|access-date=November 16, 2020|archive-date=January 8, 2021|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20210108133440/https://watch.cjenm.com/broadcast/sub/view?topcategory=DRM10&formatYn=N&contentsId=B120209759|url-status=live}}</ref>

==Synopsis==
In the modern age, Jang Bong-hwan ([[Choi Jin-hyuk]]) is a head chef who works at the [[Blue House]]. He is a womanizer with a free spirit. One day, he gets in trouble when a Chinese delegate takes out a fish hook after biting into food cooked by him. After a near-death experience and falling into a pool, he finds himself in the body of So-yong as [[Queen Cheorin]] ([[Shin Hye-sun]]) in the [[Joseon]] period.

[[Cheoljong of Joseon|King Cheoljong]] ([[Kim Jung-hyun (actor, born 1990)|Kim Jung-hyun]]), the reigning monarch, is a gentle and easy-going person. However, he is a king as a figurehead, while the true power is wielded by the late [[Queen Cheorin|King Sunjo]]'s widow, [[Queen Sunwon]] ([[Bae Jong-ok]]). Bong-hwan goes on a mission to win Queen Sunwon's favor by cooking for her, and to try to return to his body in modern Korea. However, Bong-hwan soon discovers that So-yong has a secret, while the King is not what he seems and has a dark and suspicious side to him.<ref>{{cite web|title=申惠善&金正賢搭檔魂穿劇《哲仁王后》 當現代男人靈魂穿進古代中殿身體里|trans-title=Shin Hye Sun & Kim Jong Hyun partner in the soul wear drama "Queen Che In" When the soul of modern men penetrates the body of the ancient nave|url=https://www.koreastardaily.com/tc/news/127296|publisher=Korea Star Daily|language=zh-hant|date=June 5, 2020|access-date=October 20, 2020|archive-date=October 21, 2020|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20201021204353/https://www.koreastardaily.com/tc/news/127296|url-status=live}}</ref>{{Unreliable source?|reason=Per [[WP:KO/RS#UR]]|date=July 2024}}

In October, the first script reading was held.<ref>{{Cite web|script-title=ko:김정현X신혜선, 조선의 쇼윈도 부부...'철인왕후', 유쾌한 대본리딩|url=https://www.dispatch.co.kr/2110896|website=디스패치 &#124; 뉴스는 팩트다!|date=October 28, 2020|access-date=November 14, 2020|archive-date=November 24, 2020|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20201124103632/https://www.dispatch.co.kr/2110896|url-status=live}}</ref> The stills from filming of the series were released on November 16, 2020.<ref>{{Cite web|script-title=ko:'철인왕후' 신혜선x김정현 첫 스틸...조선판 쇼윈도 부부 "신들린 코믹"|url=https://n.news.naver.com/entertain/article/421/0004991657|website=[[Naver]]|last=Jang|first=Ah-reum|date=November 16, 2020|access-date=November 16, 2020|archive-date=November 16, 2020|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20201116003138/https://n.news.naver.com/entertain/article/421/0004991657|url-status=live}}</ref>

The series reunites actors [[Kim Jung-hyun (actor, born 1990)|Kim Jung-hyun]] and [[Seol In-ah]] who previously acted together in the drama ''[[School 2017]]''.

It also marks the second collaboration of [[Shin Hye-sun]] with actors [[Bae Jong-ok]], [[Kim In-kwon]] and {{ill|Lee Jae-won (actor)|lt=Lee Jae-won|ko|이재원 (배우)}} with whom she acted in the film ''[[Innocence (2020 film)|Innocence]]'' and the dramas ''[[Angel's Last Mission: Love]]'' and ''[[Legend of the Blue Sea]]'', respectively.

==Reception==
===Controversies===
Following its airing, a controversy erupted in episode 2 as the series referred to Korea's national treasure, the [[Veritable Records of the Joseon Dynasty]] as a "[[Tabloid (newspaper format)|tabloid]]." This drew outrage among viewers and about 700 people filed complaints to the [[Korea Communications Standards Commission]].<ref>{{Cite web|title='Mr. Queen' rating continues to rise despite criticism for 'distorting history'|url=https://www.koreatimes.co.kr/www/art/2021/01/688_301524.html|website=[[The Korea Times]]|language=en|date=December 27, 2020|access-date=January 3, 2021|archive-date=February 5, 2021|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20210205020305/http://www.koreatimes.co.kr/www/art/2021/01/688_301524.html|url-status=live}}</ref> The series was also criticised as the author of the original Chinese drama has made negative remarks about Korea in their other work.<ref>{{Cite web|title=Shin Hye Sun and Kim Jung Hyun's K Drama Mr Queen's ratings and viewership on the rise despite controversies|url=https://www.ibtimes.sg/shin-hye-sun-kim-jung-hyuns-mr-queens-ratings-rise-despite-controversies-54325|website=www.ibtimes.sg|language=en|date=December 20, 2020|access-date=January 3, 2021|archive-date=December 20, 2020|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20201220064811/https://www.ibtimes.sg/shin-hye-sun-kim-jung-hyuns-mr-queens-ratings-rise-despite-controversies-54325|url-status=live}}</ref>{{Unreliable source?|reason=Per [[WP:IBTIMES]]|date=July 2024}} The producers released a statement clarifying their stance and apologising for the controversies, adding that they were not aware of the negative comments made by the novelist.<ref>{{Cite web|title=Mr. Queen in controversy over OG novelist and The Annals of the Joseon Dynasty line; Makers issue apology|url=https://www.pinkvilla.com/entertainment/mr-queen-controversy-over-og-novelist-and-annals-joseon-dynasty-line-makers-issue-apology-584197|website=PINKVILLA|language=en|access-date=January 3, 2021|archive-date=December 16, 2020|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20201216125443/https://www.pinkvilla.com/entertainment/mr-queen-controversy-over-og-novelist-and-annals-joseon-dynasty-line-makers-issue-apology-584197|url-status=live}}</ref>

===Global audience viewership===
''Mr. Queen'' became the 4th most popular TV show on Netflix a few days after it was made available for streaming on February 20, 2023, as per data provided by FlixPatrol for February 20. ''Mr. Queen'' went further up the Netflix Top 10 TV Shows Worldwide Chart.<ref>{{Cite web|author1=Eunice |title=High-Rating tvN K-Drama "Mr. Queen" Rises On The Netflix Worldwide Chart 2 Years After Its TV Broadcast|url=https://www.kpopmap.com/high-rating-tvn-k-drama-mr-queen-rises-on-the-netflix-worldwide-chart-2-years-after-its-tv-broadcast/|website=[[Kpopmap]]|language=en|date=February 20, 2023|access-date=February 24, 2023|archive-date=February 24, 2023|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20230224135347/https://www.kpopmap.com/high-rating-tvn-k-drama-mr-queen-rises-on-the-netflix-worldwide-chart-2-years-after-its-tv-broadcast/|url-status=live}}</ref>{{Unreliable source?|reason=Per [[WP:KO/RS#UR]]|date=July 2024}}

''Mr. Queen'' took 5th place in the Netflix global viewing hours with 20.9 million viewing hours in its first week after it was made available for streaming on Netflix. It reached Top 10 in TV in 33 countries from  [[Africa]], [[Asia]], and the [[Americas]] including Brazil, Bolivia, Ecuador, Mexico, Peru, Kenya, Morocco, Nigeria, Australia, New Zealand, Bangladesh, [[Hong Kong]], Indonesia, India, Malaysia, Pakistan, Philippines, Saudi Arabia, Singapore, South Korea, Sri Lanka, Taiwan, Thailand, Turkey, and Vietnam.<ref>{{Cite web|title=Unlocked (2023), Mr. Queen (2020) Enter Netflix Global Top 10|url=https://korean-binge.com/2023/02/22/unlocked-2023-mr-queen-2020-enter-netflix-global-top-10/|website=[[Kpopmap]]|language=en|date=February 21, 2023|access-date=February 24, 2023|archive-date=February 24, 2023|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20230224135556/https://korean-binge.com/2023/02/22/unlocked-2023-mr-queen-2020-enter-netflix-global-top-10/|url-status=live}}</ref>
