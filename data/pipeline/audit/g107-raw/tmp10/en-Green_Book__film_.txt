'''''Green Book''''' is a 2018 American [[comedy-drama]] film directed by [[Peter Farrelly]]. Starring [[Viggo Mortensen]] and [[Mahershala Ali]], the [[Biographical film|biographical]] film is inspired by the true story of a 1962 tour of the [[Deep South]] by African-American pianist [[Don Shirley]] and Italian-American bouncer and later actor [[Tony Lip|Frank "Tony Lip" Vallelonga]], who served as Shirley's driver and bodyguard. Written by Farrelly alongside Lip's son [[Nick Vallelonga]] and [[Brian Hayes Currie]], the film is based on interviews with Lip and Shirley, as well as letters Lip wrote to his wife.<ref>{{cite web |last1=Diamond |first1=Anna |title=The True Story of the 'Green Book' Movie |url=https://www.smithsonianmag.com/arts-culture/true-story-green-book-movie-180970728/ |website=Smithsonian.com |publisher=Smithsonian Magazine |access-date=December 2, 2018 |date=December 2018}}</ref> It is named after ''[[The Negro Motorist Green Book]]'', a guide book for African-American travelers founded by [[Victor Hugo Green]] in 1936 and published until 1966.

''Green Book'' had its world premiere at the [[Toronto International Film Festival]] on September 11, 2018, where it won the [[Toronto International Film Festival People's Choice Award|People's Choice Award]]. It was then theatrically released in the United States on November 16, 2018, by [[Universal Pictures]], and grossed $321 million worldwide. The film received positive reviews from critics, with praise for the performances of Mortensen and Ali, although it also drew some criticism for its racial dynamics and its depiction of Shirley.

''Green Book'' received many [[List of accolades received by Green Book|awards and nominations]]. It won the [[Academy Award]] for [[Academy Award for Best Picture|Best Picture]], [[Academy Award for Best Original Screenplay|Best Original Screenplay]], and [[Academy Award for Best Supporting Actor|Best Supporting Actor]] (for Ali), making it the fifth film to win Best Picture without being nominated for Best Director (after ''[[Wings (1927 film)|Wings]]'', ''[[Grand Hotel (1932 film)|Grand Hotel]]'', ''[[Driving Miss Daisy]]'' and ''[[Argo (2012 film)|Argo]]''). It also won the [[Producers Guild of America Award for Best Theatrical Motion Picture]], the [[Golden Globe Award for Best Motion Picture – Musical or Comedy]], and the [[National Board of Review Awards 2018|National Board of Review]] award for the [[National Board of Review Award for Best Film|best film of 2018]], and was chosen as one of the top 10 films of the year by the [[American Film Institute Awards 2018|American Film Institute]]. Ali also won the [[Golden Globe Award for Best Supporting Actor – Motion Picture|Golden Globe]], [[Screen Actors Guild Award for Outstanding Performance by a Male Actor in a Supporting Role|Screen Actors Guild]], and [[BAFTA Award for Best Actor in a Supporting Role|BAFTA Awards]] for Best Supporting Actor.<ref>{{Cite web |date=January 6, 2019 |title=Mahershala Ali wins Best Supporting Actor Golden Globe for 'Green Book' |url=https://ew.com/golden-globes/2019/01/06/mahershala-ali-wins-best-supporting-actor-golden-globe-green-book/ |access-date=January 17, 2019 |website=EW.com |language=en}}</ref><ref>{{Cite web|url=https://www.hollywoodreporter.com/news/mahershala-ali-wins-first-golden-globe-green-book-1173722|title=Mahershala Ali Wins First Golden Globe for 'Green Book'|website=The Hollywood Reporter|date=January 6, 2019 |language=en|access-date=January 17, 2019}}</ref>

==Plot==
<!-- Per WP:FILMPLOT, plot summaries for feature films should be set between 400-700 words. -->
In the autumn of 1962, in the [[Bronx]], Italian-American bouncer [[Tony Lip]] searches for new employment while the [[Copacabana (nightclub)|Copacabana]] is closed for renovations. He is invited to an interview with [[Don Shirley]], an African American pianist in need of a driver for his eight-week concert tour through the [[Midwest]] and [[Deep South]].

Don hires Tony on the strength of his references. They embark with plans to return to [[New York City]] on [[Christmas Eve]]. Don's record label gives Tony a copy of ''[[The Negro Motorist Green Book]]'', a guide for African American travelers that contains the addresses of those motels, restaurants, and filling stations that would serve them in the [[Jim Crow laws|Jim Crow South]].<ref name="book">{{cite web |date=January 20, 2019 |title=The Green Book |url=https://digitalcollections.nypl.org/collections/the-green-book#/?tab=about |access-date=January 20, 2019 |work=[[New York Public Library|NYPL Digital Library]]}}</ref>

Tony and Don clash as Tony feels uncomfortable being asked to act with more refinement, while haughty Don is displeased by Tony's uncouth habits. As the tour progresses, Tony is impressed with Don's talent on the piano and becomes increasingly appalled by the discriminatory treatment Don receives from his hosts and the general public when he is not on stage.

In [[Louisville, Kentucky]], a group of white men beat Don and threaten him at gunpoint before Tony rescues him. He instructs Don not to go out without him for the rest of the tour.

Throughout the journey, Don helps Tony write eloquent letters to his wife, which deeply move her. Tony encourages Don to get in touch with his own estranged brother, but Don is hesitant, observing he has become isolated by his professional life and achievements. Don is later found in a homosexual encounter with a white man in a public shower, and Tony, unfazed, bribes the police officers at the scene to prevent his arrest.

In [[Mississippi]], the two are arrested after police officers pull them over late at night in a [[sundown town]], and Tony punches one after being insulted. While in jail, Don asks to phone his lawyer,  but calls [[United States Attorney General|Attorney General]] [[Robert F. Kennedy]], who pressures the governor and police to release them.

Once they resume traveling, Don reprimands Tony for his actions, and a heated argument erupts regarding race relations and [[meritocracy]], with Don expressing frustration at feeling rejected by the black community due to his mannerisms while the white community either mistreats him or uses him to make themselves look open-minded. They eventually find a hotel for the night and reconcile.

On the night of Don's final performance in [[Birmingham, Alabama]], he is refused entry into the whites-only dining room of the [[country club]] where he is due to perform. Tony threatens the manager, and Don refuses to play unless they serve him in the room with his audience. Eventually, Don gives Tony the choice on whether or not he plays, to smooth tensions. Tony chooses his friendship, refusing bribery from the manager, possibly terminating his contract with the record company that demands Don does not miss a show. Tony and Don leave the venue and instead eat at a black blues club, where Don joins the band on piano. As the pair leave the club, Tony senses and scares off two patrons with a firearm who were intending to ambush them for Don's money, which he had displayed earlier, an action which visibly startles Don.

The pair head north, attempting to get home by Christmas Eve, but are caught in a blizzard. They are then once again pulled over by a police officer. Worried they will receive the same treatment as earlier, both are surprised when it turns out the officer had only pulled them over due to a flat tire, which he kindly helps them fix. Continuing back to New York, Tony, for reasons of sleep deprivation, decides he is unable to continue driving, meaning he will miss Christmas dinner. Don, not wanting this to happen, drives for him, with Tony sleeping in the backseat.

Tony invites Don to have dinner with his family, but Don declines. At the party, a relative of Tony makes a racial remark, which Tony objects to, leading to the relative's acquiescence. Sitting alone at home, Don changes his mind and returns to Tony's, where he receives a warm welcome from Tony's extended family.

The end title cards show real-life photos of Don and Tony, explaining Don continued to tour and create music, while Tony went back working at the Copacabana, and they remained friends until dying months apart in 2013.

=== Music ===
{{Main|Green Book (soundtrack){{!}}''Green Book'' (soundtrack)}}
For the film's soundtrack, Farrelly incorporated an original score by composer [[Kris Bowers]] and one of Shirley's own recordings. The soundtrack also includes rarities from 1950s and 1960s American music recommended to him by singer [[Robert Plant]], who was dating a friend of Farrelly's wife at the time he had finished the film's script. During dinner on a double date, his wife and her friend stepped outside to smoke and the director asked Plant for advice on picking songs for the film that would be relatively unknown to contemporary audiences. This prompted Plant to play Farrelly songs via [[YouTube]], including [[Sonny Boy Williamson II]]'s "Pretty 'Lil Thing" and [[Robert Mosley (pop musician)|Robert Mosley]]'s "Goodbye, My Lover, Goodbye".<ref>{{cite magazine| url=https://www.forbes.com/sites/stevebaltin/2019/02/16/robert-plants-friendly-role-in-green-book-soundtrack-and-other-behind-the-scenes-secrets/?sh=fade49f2e070|first=Steve|last=Baltin|magazine=[[Forbes Magazine]]|publisher=|title=Robert Plant's Friendly Role In 'Green Book' Soundtrack And Other Behind The Scenes Secrets|date=16 February 2019|accessdate=10 November 2021}}</ref>

In an interview with ''[[Forbes]]'', the director explained the soundtrack ended up not only avoiding rote nostalgia, "but also those songs were really inexpensive and I did not have a huge budget so I was able to come up with some sensational pop songs from the time that were long forgotten."<ref>{{cite news|last=Lifton|first=Dave|date=February 18, 2019|url=http://ultimateclassicrock.com/robert-plant-green-book-soundtrack/|title=How Robert Plant Helped Curate the 'Green Book' Soundtrack|website=[[Ultimate Classic Rock]]|access-date=February 21, 2019}}</ref> The music played at the black blues club toward the end of the film featured the piano performance of ''[[Étude Op. 25, No. 11 (Chopin)]]'', known as the ''Winter Wind'' etude by Chopin. This was not included in the soundtrack release.

A soundtrack album was released digitally on November 16, 2018, and physically on November 30, 2018,<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.allmusic.com/album/green-book-mw0003218440|title=Green Book – Kris Bowers|website=[[AllMusic]]|access-date=February 21, 2019}}</ref> by [[Milan Records]],<ref name="bb"/> featuring Bowers' score, songs from the plot's era, and a piano recording by Shirley.<ref>{{cite web|last=Bundel|first=Ani|date=December 2018|url=https://www.elitedaily.com/p/the-green-book-soundtrack-will-fill-you-with-so-much-joy-13260064|title=The 'Green Book' Soundtrack Will Fill You With So Much Joy|website=[[Elite Daily]]|access-date=February 21, 2019}}</ref> According to the label, it was [[music streaming|streamed]] approximately 10,000 times per day during January 2019. This rate doubled the next month as the album surpassed one million streams worldwide and became the highest-streamed jazz soundtrack on the Milan label.<ref name="bb">{{cite magazine|last=Reitman|first=Shelby|date=February 13, 2019|url=https://www.billboard.com/articles/news/8498128/green-book-official-soundtrack-reaches-1-million-global-streams|title='Green Book' Official Soundtrack Passes 1 Million Global Streams|magazine=[[Billboard (magazine)|Billboard]]|access-date=February 21, 2019}}</ref>

==Release==
Originally in production at [[Focus Features]], the company ultimately passed on the project. [[Participant Media]] then financed the $23 million budget for ''Green Book'', but Farrelly was concerned about the fact that Participant had a distribution deal with Focus. Knowing very well about [[Steven Spielberg]] and his studio's longtime relationship with [[Universal Pictures]], Farrelly decided to call his agent Richard Lovett, who also represents Spielberg. Lovett managed to convince Spielberg to watch the film, which he loved so much he reportedly watched it five times over two weeks, calling it "his favorite [[Buddy film|buddy movie]] since ''[[Butch Cassidy and the Sundance Kid]]''". Spielberg then took the film to [[DreamWorks Pictures]], which currently has a domestic distribution deal with Universal.<ref>{{cite web|title=How Steven Spielberg Changed The Course For 'Green Book' – Awardsline Screening Series|website=[[Deadline Hollywood]]|first=Anthony|last=D'Alessandro|date=14 February 2019|access-date=16 May 2024|url=https://deadline.com/2019/02/green-book-steven-spielberg-peter-farrelly-1202557722/}}</ref>

''Green Book'' began a limited release in 20 cities, in the United States, on November 16, 2018, and expanded nationwide on November 21, 2018. The film was previously scheduled to begin its release on the 21st.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://deadline.com/2018/10/green-book-going-earlier-in-limited-release-off-awards-season-heat-1202493154/|title='Green Book' Going Earlier In Limited Release Off Awards Season Heat|website=[[Deadline Hollywood]]|publisher=[[Penske Business Media]]|date=October 31, 2018 |first=Anthony |last= D'Alessandro|access-date=October 31, 2018}}</ref> The studio spent an estimated $37.5 million on prints and advertisements for the film.<ref name=profit/>

The film had its world premiere at the [[Toronto International Film Festival]] on September 11, 2018.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.screendaily.com/news/toronto-unveils-contemporary-world-cinema-more-galas-and-special-presentations-/5131634.article|title=Toronto unveils Contemporary World Cinema, more Galas and Special Presentations|website=[[Screen International]]|first=Jeremy|last=Kay|date=August 14, 2018|access-date=August 14, 2018}}</ref> It also opened the 29th [[New Orleans Film Festival]] on October 17, 2018, screened at [[AFI Fest]] on November 9, 2018<ref>{{cite web|url=https://deadline.com/2018/10/afi-fest-adds-gala-screenings-green-book-widows-world-premiere-of-netflixs-bird-box-with-sandra-bullock-and-the-kominsky-method-tv-series-1202481023/|title=AFI Fest Adds Gala Screenings 'Green Book', 'Widows', World Premiere Of Netflix's 'Bird Box' With Sandra Bullock and 'The Kominsky Method' TV Series|website=[[Deadline Hollywood]]|first=Pete|last=Hammond|date=October 11, 2018|access-date=October 11, 2018}}</ref> and was programmed as the surprise film at the [[BFI London Film Festival]].<ref name="Filmoria">{{cite web |date=16 October 2018 |title=Green Book – Watch New Clip |url=https://www.filmoria.co.uk/green-book-watch-new-clip/ |access-date=October 16, 2018 |website=Filmoria.co.uk}}</ref>

On November 7, 2018, during a promotional panel discussion, Mortensen said the word "[[nigger]]". He prefaced the sentence with, "I don't like saying this word", and went on to compare dialogue "that's no longer common in conversation" to the period in which the film is set. Mortensen apologized the next day, saying "my intention was to speak strongly against racism" and he was "very sorry that I did use the full word last night, and will not utter it again".<ref>{{cite news|url=https://variety.com/2018/film/news/viggo-mortensen-n-word-green-book-1203024519/|title=Viggo Mortensen Apologizes for Using N-Word at 'Green Book' Panel|work=[[Variety (magazine)|Variety]]|first1=Matt |last1=Donnelly |first2=Meredith |last2=Woerner |date=November 9, 2018|access-date=November 14, 2018}}</ref>

==Reception==
===Box office===
''Green Book'' grossed $85.1 million in the United States and Canada, and $236.7 million in other territories, for a total worldwide gross of $321.8 million, against a production budget of $23 million.<ref name=NUM/> ''[[Deadline Hollywood]]'' calculated the net profit of the film to be $106 million, when factoring together all expenses and revenues.<ref name=profit>{{cite magazine|url=https://deadline.com/2019/04/best-movie-profit-2018-green-book-halloween-a-quiet-place-the-nun-crazy-rich-asians-1202589429/|title=Small Movies, Big Profits: 2018 Most Valuable Blockbuster Tournament|first=Anthony|last=D'Alessandro|magazine=[[Deadline Hollywood]]|date=April 8, 2019|access-date=April 8, 2019}}</ref>

The film made $312,000 from 25 theaters in its opening weekend, an average of $12,480 per venue, which ''Deadline Hollywood'' called "not good at all", although ''[[TheWrap]]'' said it was a "successful start," and noted strong word-of-mouth would likely help it going into its wide release.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://deadline.com/2018/11/fantastic-beasts-the-crimes-of-grindelwald-weekend-box-office-opening-widows-viola-davis-green-book-1202503142/|title='Crimes Of Grindelwald' Falls Short Stateside With $62M+ Debut, WB Celebrates Global Win As 'Fantastic Beasts' Series Hits $1B-Plus|website=[[Deadline Hollywood]]|date=November 18, 2018|first=Anthony|last= D'Alessandro|access-date=November 18, 2018}}</ref><ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.thewrap.com/green-book-indie-box-office-successful-start/|title='Green Book' Has Successful Start at Indie Box Office Before Wide Release|website=[[TheWrap]]|date=November 18, 2018|first=Jeremy|last= Fuster|access-date=November 18, 2018}}</ref> The film had its wide expansion alongside the openings of ''[[Ralph Breaks the Internet]]'', ''[[Robin Hood (2018 film)|Robin Hood]]'' and ''[[Creed II]]'', and was projected to gross around $7–9 million over the five-day weekend, November 21 to 25.<ref>{{cite web |url=https://deadline.com/2018/11/ralph-breaks-the-internet-creed-ii-robin-hood-thanksgiving-box-office-projections-1202493906/|title=Ralph Breaks The Internet' Tracking To $65M; 'Creed II' Eyeing $48M, 'Robin Hood' $17M In Thanksgiving B.O. Showdown|work=[[Deadline Hollywood]]|first=Anthony|last=D'Alessandro|date=November 1, 2018|access-date=November 1, 2018}}</ref> It made $908,000 on its first day of wide release and $1 million on its second. It grossed $5.4 million over the three-day weekend (and $7.4 million over the five), finishing ninth. ''Deadline'' wrote the opening was "far from where [it needed] to be to be considered a success," and strong audience word of mouth and impending award nominations would be needed in order to help the film develop box office legs. Rival studios argued Universal went too wide too fast: from 25 theaters to 1,063 in less than a week.<ref name="opening">{{cite web|url=https://deadline.com/2018/11/ralph-breaks-the-internet-creed-ii-thanksgiving-box-office-1202505947/|title='Ralph' Breaking The B.O. With $18.5M Weds., Potential Record $95M Five-Day; 'Creed II' Pumping $11.6M Opening Day, $61M Five-Day|website=[[Deadline Hollywood]]|date=November 25, 2018|first=Anthony|last= D'Alessandro|access-date=November 25, 2018}}</ref>

In its second weekend the film made $3.9 million, falling just 29% and leading some industry insiders to think it would achieve $50 million during [[2018–19 film awards season|awards season]].<ref name="dead2">{{cite web|url=https://deadline.com/2018/12/the-possession-of-hannah-grace-grabs-625k-in-thursday-night-previews-1202511705/|title='Ralph' Breaking $25M+ 2nd Weekend; 'Grinch' Steals $202M+; 'Hannah Grace' $6M+ In Slow Post Thanksgiving Period – Update|website=[[Deadline Hollywood]]|date=December 2, 2018|first=Anthony|last= D'Alessandro|access-date=December 3, 2018}}</ref> In its third weekend of wide release, following its Golden Globe nominations, it dropped 0% and again made $3.9 million, then made $2.8 million the following weekend.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://deadline.com/2018/12/ralph-breaks-the-internet-the-favourite-emma-stone-rachel-weisz-weekend-box-office-1202516270/|title='Ralph' Keeps No. 1 Away From Greedy 'Grinch' For Third Weekend In A Row With $16M+ – Sunday Update|website=[[Deadline Hollywood]]|date=December 9, 2018|first=Anthony|last= D'Alessandro|access-date=December 9, 2018}}</ref><ref>{{cite web|first1=Anthony|last1=D'Alessandro|title='Spider-Verse' Raises $35M+ As 'The Mule' Kicks Up $17M+ In Pre-Christmas Period, But 'Mortal Engines' Breaks Down With $7M+ |url=https://deadline.com/2018/12/spider-man-into-the-spider-verse-mortal-engines-clint-eastwood-the-mule-weekend-box-office-1202520137/|website=[[Deadline Hollywood]]|date=December 16, 2018|access-date=December 17, 2018}}</ref> In its eighth weekend, the film made $1.8 million (continuing to hold well, dropping just 3% from the previous week).<ref>{{cite web |url=https://deadline.com/2019/01/aquaman-escape-room-weekend-box-office-first-weekend-2019-1202529013/|title='Aquaman' Still The Big Man At The B.O. With $30M+; 'Escape Room' Packs In $17M+ – Early Sunday Update|website=[[Deadline Hollywood]] |date=January 6, 2018|first=Anthony |last=D'Alessandro |access-date=January 6, 2019}}</ref> It then made $2.1 million in its ninth weekend (up 18%) and $2.1 million in its 10th.<ref>{{cite web |url=https://deadline.com/2019/01/glass-m-night-shyamalan-opening-weekend-box-office-1202537391/|title='Glass' Now Looking At Third-Best MLK Weekend Opening With $47M+|website=[[Deadline Hollywood]] |date=January 20, 2018|first=Anthony |last=D'Alessandro |access-date=January 20, 2019}}</ref> In the film's 11th week of release, following the announcement of its five Oscar nominations, it was added to 1,518 theaters (for a total of 2,430) and made $5.4 million, an increase of 150% from the previous weekend and finishing sixth at the box office.<ref>{{cite web |url=https://deadline.com/2019/01/box-office-glass-anne-hathaway-matthew-mcconaughey-serenity-bombs-oscars-best-picture-1202542005/|title='Glass' Leads Again At Weekend B.O., But Only A Handful Of Oscar Best Picture Noms Will See Boost|website=[[Deadline Hollywood]] |date=January 27, 2018|first=Anthony |last=D'Alessandro |access-date=January 27, 2019}}</ref> The weekend following its Best Picture win, the film was added to 1,388 theaters (for a total of 2,641) and made $4.7 million, finishing fifth at the box office. It marked a 121% increase from the previous week, as well as one of the best post-Best Picture win bumps ever,<ref>{{cite magazine|url=https://variety.com/2020/film/box-office/parasite-oscar-box-office-1203505355/|date=16 February 2020|title=Parasite' Enjoys Record Box Office Boost After Oscar Wins|magazine=[[Variety (magazine)|Variety]]|first=Rebecca|last=Rubin|publisher=Variety Media, LLC|accessdate=10 November 2021}}</ref> and largest since ''[[The King's Speech]]'' in 2011.<ref>{{cite news |last1=D'Alessandro |first1=Anthony |title='Dragon 3' Keeps The Fire Burning At No. 1 With $30M Second Weekend; 'Madea' Mints $27M|url=https://deadline.com/2019/03/madea-famliy-funeral-how-to-train-your-dragon-weekend-box-office-1202567796/|access-date=March 3, 2019 |work=[[Deadline Hollywood]] |date=March 3, 2019}}</ref>

''Green Book'' was a surprise success overseas, especially in China where it debuted to a much higher-than-expected $17.3 million, immediately becoming the second highest-grossing Best Picture winner in the country behind ''[[Titanic (1997 film)|Titanic]]'' (1997).<ref>{{cite news |date=March 9, 2019 |title=Green Book enjoys box office boost after Oscar win |work=Film Industry Network |url=https://filmindustry.network/green-book-enjoys-box-office-boost-after-oscar-win/34305}}</ref> As of March 7, 2019, the largest international markets for the film were China ($26.7 million), France ($10.7 million), the United Kingdom ($10 million), Australia ($7.8 million) and Italy ($8.6 million).<ref name=INTgross>{{cite news |last1=Tartaglione|first1=Nancy|title='Green Book' Drives Past $200M Worldwide; Oscar Winner At $27M In China|url=https://deadline.com/2019/03/green-book-milestone-200-million-worldwide-box-office-china-1202571429/|access-date=March 7, 2019 |work=[[Deadline Hollywood]] |date=March 7, 2019}}</ref> By March 13, China's total had grown to $44.5 million.<ref>{{cite web |url=https://www.latimes.com/entertainment/la-fi-ct-box-office-captain-marvel-wonder-park-20190313-story.html|title='Captain Marvel' is likely to crush 'Wonder Park' at the box office|website=[[Los Angeles Times]] |date=March 14, 2018|first=Ryan |last=Faughnder |access-date=March 14, 2019}}</ref> On March 31 the film passed $300 million at the global box office, including $219 million from overseas territories. Its largest markets to-date were China ($70.7 million), Japan ($14.6 million), France ($14 million) Germany ($13.5 million) and the UK ($12.9 million).<ref>{{cite news |last1=Tartaglione|first1=Nancy|title='Green Book' Logs $300M+ At Worldwide Box Office; Tops $70M In China|url=https://deadline.com/2019/04/green-book-crosses-300-million-worldwide-box-office-china-1202586498/|access-date=April 1, 2019 |work=[[Deadline Hollywood]] |date=April 1, 2019}}</ref>

===Critical response===
[[File:Mahershala Ali (29953410761).jpg|thumb|upright|Mahershala Ali's performance as [[Don Shirley]] received positive reviews, earning him his second [[Academy Award for Best Supporting Actor]].]]
On [[review aggregator]] [[Rotten Tomatoes]], ''Green Book'' holds an approval rating of {{RT data|score}} based on {{RT data|count}} reviews, with an average rating of {{RT data|average}}. The website's critical consensus reads: "''Green Book'' takes audiences on an excessively smooth ride through bumpy subject matter, although Mahershala Ali and Viggo Mortensen's performances add necessary depth."<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.rottentomatoes.com/m/green_book|title=Green Book (2018)|website=[[Rotten Tomatoes]]|publisher=[[Fandango Media|Fandango]]|access-date={{RT data|access date}}}}</ref> On [[Metacritic]] the film has a [[Weighted arithmetic mean|weighted average score]] of 69 out of 100, based on 52 critics, indicating "generally favorable" reviews.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.metacritic.com/movie/green-book|title=Green Book reviews|website=[[Metacritic]]|access-date=February 9, 2019}}</ref> Audiences polled by [[CinemaScore]] gave the film a rare average grade of "A+" on an A+ to F scale, while [[PostTrak]] reported filmgoers gave it a 91% positive score, with 80% saying they would definitely recommend it.<ref name="opening"/><ref>{{cite web |url=https://deadline.com/2019/02/oscars-best-picture-box-office-black-panther-roma-green-book-1202562777/|title='Which Film Stands To Gain The Most At The B.O. From An Oscar Best Picture Win? Perhaps None Of Them|website=[[Deadline Hollywood]] |date=February 22, 2018|first=Anthony |last=D'Alessandro |access-date=February 23, 2019}}</ref>

Writing for ''[[The San Francisco Chronicle]]'', [[Mick LaSalle]] praised Ali and Mortensen and said: "...there's something so deeply right about this movie, so true to the time depicted and so welcome in this moment; so light in its touch, so properly respectful of its characters, and so big in its spirit,                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                   the movie acquires a glow. It achieves that glow slowly, but by the middle and certainly by the end, it's there, the sense of something magical happening, on screen and within the audience."<ref>{{cite web|last=LaSalle|first=Mike|title=Viggo Mortensen and Mahershala Ali achieve screen magic in 'Green Book'|url=https://datebook.sfchronicle.com/movies-tv/viggo-mortensen-and-mahershala-ali-achieve-screen-magic-in-green-book|date=November 14, 2018|work=[[San Francisco Chronicle]]|access-date=November 22, 2018}}</ref> Steve Pond of ''[[TheWrap]]'' wrote, "The movie gets darker as the journey goes further South, and as the myriad indignities and humiliations mount. But our investment in the characters rarely flags, thanks to Mortensen and Ali and a director who is interested in cleanly and efficiently delivering a story worth hearing."<ref>{{cite web|last=Pond|first=Steve|title='Green Book' Film Review: Viggo Mortensen and Mahershala Ali Take a Perilous Road Trip Through the Deep South|url=https://www.thewrap.com/green-book-film-review-viggo-mortensen-mahershala-ali-deep-south/|date=November 14, 2018|work=[[TheWrap]]|access-date=November 22, 2018}}</ref>

Jazz artist [[Quincy Jones]] said to a crowd after a screening: "I had the pleasure of being acquainted with Don Shirley while I was working as an arranger in New York in the '50s, and he was without question one of America's greatest pianists&nbsp;... as skilled a musician as [[Leonard Bernstein]] or [[Van Cliburn]]&nbsp;... So it is wonderful that his story is finally being told and celebrated. Mahershala, you did an absolutely fantastic job playing him, and I think yours and Viggo's performances will go down as one of the great friendships captured on film."<ref name='Jones'>{{cite web|url=https://deadline.com/2018/12/julia-roberts-quincy-jones-green-book-steven-spielberg-1202520537/|title=Notes On The Season: Quincy Jones Loves 'Green Book'|date=December 15, 2018|work=[[Deadline Hollywood]]|first=Pete|last=Hammond|access-date=December 17, 2018}}</ref>

Some critics thought ''Green Book'' perpetuated racial stereotyping by advancing the [[white savior narrative in film]]. ''[[Salon (website)|Salon]]'' said the film combines "the white savior trope with the story of a bigot's redemption."<ref>{{Cite web|url=https://www.salon.com/2018/12/30/hollywood-still-loves-a-white-savior-green-book-and-the-lazy-feel-good-take-on-race/|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20181230232333/https://www.salon.com/2018/12/30/hollywood-still-loves-a-white-savior-green-book-and-the-lazy-feel-good-take-on-race/|url-status=live|archive-date=December 30, 2018|title=Hollywood still loves a white savior: "Green Book" and the lazy, feel-good take on race|last=McFarland|first=Melanie|date=December 30, 2018|website=[[Salon (website)|Salon]]|language=en|access-date=March 1, 2019}}</ref><ref name="EW -Green Book best picture">{{cite magazine|last=Nolfi|first=Joey|date=January 6, 2019|title=''Green Book'' wins Best Picture — Comedy or Musical at Golden Globes|url=https://ew.com/golden-globes/2019/01/06/golden-globes-2019-best-picture-musical-comedy-winner/|magazine=[[Entertainment Weekly]]|access-date=February 4, 2019}}</ref> Peter Farrelly told ''[[Entertainment Weekly]]'' that he was aware of the white savior trope before filming and sought to avoid it. He said he had long discussions with the actors and producers on the point, and believes it was not advanced by the film, saying it is "about two guys who were complete opposites and found a common ground, and it's not one guy saving the other. It's both saving each other and pulling each other into some place where they could bond and form a lifetime friendship."<ref name="EW -Green Book best picture" />

''[[The New York Times|New York Times]]'' writer [[Wesley Morris]] characterized the film as being a "racial reconciliation fantasy", arguing it represents a specific style of racial storytelling "in which the wheels of interracial friendship are greased by employment, in which prolonged exposure to the black half of the duo enhances the humanity of his white, frequently racist counterpart".<ref>{{Cite news|url=https://www.nytimes.com/2019/01/23/arts/green-book-interracial-friendship.html|title=Why Do the Oscars Keep Falling for Racial Reconciliation Fantasies?|last=Morris|first=Wesley|date=January 23, 2019|work=The New York Times|access-date=March 2, 2019|language=en-US|issn=0362-4331|author-link=Wesley Morris}}</ref> Writing a positive review of the film in ''[[The Hollywood Reporter]],'' [[Kareem Abdul-Jabbar]] noted "filmmakers are history's interpreters, not its chroniclers."<ref name=":0">{{Cite news|url=https://www.hollywoodreporter.com/news/kareem-abdul-jabbar-truth-green-book-controversy-1175540|title=Kareem Abdul-Jabbar: Why the 'Green Book' Controversies Don't Matter|last=Abdul-Jabbar, Kareem|date=January 14, 2019|work=The Hollywood Reporter|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20190115015753/https://www.hollywoodreporter.com/news/kareem-abdul-jabbar-truth-green-book-controversy-1175540|archive-date=January 15, 2019|url-status=live|author-link=Kareem Abdul-Jabbar}}</ref>

Filmmaker [[Spike Lee]], whose ''[[BlacKkKlansman]]'' was also nominated for [[Academy Award for Best Picture|Best Picture]] at the [[91st Academy Awards]], expressed dissatisfaction with ''Green Book''{{'s}} win. Lee tried to exit the [[Dolby Theatre]] after the announcement of ''Green Book'''s victory, before turning his back on the acceptance speech that followed.<ref>{{cite news|title=Spike Lee Pulls A Kanye When ‘Green Book’ Takes Best Picture Oscar|first=Mike |last=Fleming Jr|date=February 24, 2019 |work=Deadline | url=https://deadline.com/2019/02/spike-lee-green-book-storms-out-oscars-blackkklansman-jordan-peele-1202564402/}}</ref><ref>{{cite news|work=Newsweek | title=‘The Ref Made a Bad Call’: Spike Lee Tried to Storm Out of Oscars After ‘Green Book’ Won Best Picture Award |date= Feb 25, 2019 |first=Ewan |last=Palmer|url=https://www.newsweek.com/oscars-spike-lee-green-book-best-picture-blackkklansman-1342481}}</ref> Speaking to the press later that night, Lee compared the film to another Best Picture winner ''[[Driving Miss Daisy]]'' (1989), lamenting its Oscar success over his film ''[[Do the Right Thing]]'' (1989). He said "I thought I was courtside at the [[Madison Square Garden|[Madison Square] Garden]], and the ref made a bad call."<ref>{{cite news |last1=Tarrant |first1=Bill |title='Ref made a bad call,' Spike Lee says of 'Green Book' award |url=https://www.reuters.com/article/lifestyle/ref-made-a-bad-call-spike-lee-says-of-green-book-award-idUSKCN1QE0SX/ |access-date=May 16, 2025 |work=[[Reuters]] |date=February 25, 2019}}</ref> Asked if the film had offended him, Lee told BBC reporters it "wasn't my cup of tea."<ref>{{cite av|title=Oscars 2019: Spike Lee says Green Book 'not my cup of tea'|url=https://www.bbc.com/news/av/entertainment-arts-47354748|work=[[BBC]]|date=February 25, 2019 |last=Lee |first=Spike |author-link=Spike Lee |type=Video |via=BBC |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20241205002331/https://www.bbc.com/news/av/entertainment-arts-47354748 |archive-date=December 5, 2024 |url-status=live}}</ref> He echoed the sentiment in a 2024 podcast, saying "no one's looking at ''Green Book'' now."<ref>{{cite podcast |last=Lee |first=Spike |author-link=Spike Lee |title=Spike Lee Talks Oscars, Jordan & Magic Funding Malcolm X, Setting Trends & Basketball Hall of Fame |url=https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Z_dMHLin3GU |work=The Realest Podcast Ever |via=[[YouTube]] |interviewer-last1=Fain |interviewer-first1=Chad |interviewer-last2=Cain |interviewer-first2=Matt |time=22:46 |quote=No one's looking at ''Green Book'' now.}}</ref>

''Green Book'' finished at number 264 in the 2025 "Readers' Choice" edition of ''[[The New York Times]]''{{'}} list of "The 100 Best Movies of the 21st Century."<ref>{{cite web |title=Readers Choose Their Top Movies of the 21st Century |url=https://www.nytimes.com/interactive/2025/movies/readers-movies-21st-century.html |website=The New York Times |date=July 2, 2025 |access-date=2 July 2025}}</ref>

===Criticism from Shirley's relatives===
Shirley's relatives thought the film misrepresented the pianist's relationship with his family, and said they were not contacted by studio representatives until after development started. Shirley's brother, Maurice, said, "My brother never considered Tony to be his 'friend'; he was an employee, his chauffeur (who resented wearing a uniform and cap). This is why context and nuance are so important. The fact that a successful, well-to-do black artist would employ domestics that did NOT look like him, should not be lost in translation."<ref name="lynn">{{cite web|url=https://www.blackenterprise.com/don-shirley-the-green-book-family-blasts-movie/|date=November 29, 2018|accessdate=July 29, 2024|title=Family of Black Man, Don Shirley, Portrayed in 'The Green Book' Blasts Movie and Its 'Lies' |work=[[Black Enterprise]]|first=Samara |last=Lynn}}</ref>  However, in audio recordings from the 2010 documentary ''Lost Bohemia'', Don Shirley said "I trusted him implicitly ... not only was he my driver. We never had an employer/employee relationship." The interviews also supported other events depicted in the film.<ref>{{Cite web|url=https://deadline.com/2019/01/green-book-tapes-doc-shirley-tony-lip-vallelonga-actual-audio-mahershala-ali-viggo-mortensen-1202541392/|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20190128105620/https://deadline.com/2019/01/green-book-tapes-doc-shirley-tony-lip-vallelonga-actual-audio-mahershala-ali-viggo-mortensen-1202541392/|archive-date=January 28, 2019|url-status=live|title=Green Book's Real Tapes: Audio Of Doc Shirley And Tony Lip Vallelonga|work=[[Deadline Hollywood]]|first=Mike Jr.|last=Fleming |date=January 28, 2019|access-date=July 29, 2024}}</ref>

Writer-director Peter Farrelly said he was under the impression there "weren't a lot of family members" still alive, they did not take major liberties with the story, and relatives of whom he was aware had been invited to a private screening for friends and family.<ref name="variety" /> Tony Vallelonga's son, Nick – the film's co-writer – acknowledged he was sorry he had offended members of the Shirley family by not speaking to them. He told ''[[Variety (magazine)|Variety]]'': "Don Shirley himself told me not to speak to anyone" and Shirley "approved what I put in and didn't put in."<ref name="auto"/>
According to Shirley's nephew Edwin Shirley III, actor Mahershala Ali called him to apologize, saying "I did the best I could with the material I had," and he was not aware there were "close relatives with whom I could have consulted to add some nuance to the character."<ref name="variety">{{cite web|url=https://www.vulture.com/2018/12/mahershala-ali-green-book-dr-don-shirley-family-apology.html|title=Mahershala Ali Apologized to His Green Book Character's Family After Controversy|date=December 15, 2018|website=[[Vulture (website)|Vulture]]|first=Hunter|last=Harris|access-date=December 17, 2018}}</ref>

Shirley's cellist Jüri Täht was surprised to see a stage of his life depicted in a movie, and that he had been replaced by a fictionalised version of himself (the character of Oleg). He was dismayed that Oleg was made to be Russian in the film whereas Täht was an Estonian whose family had had to flee the USSR.<ref>{{Cite book |last1=Täht |first1=Evelin |url=https://pappus.ee/author-evelin-taht/film-green-book/ |title=A Rediscovered Trio |last2=Mikkola |first2=Atro |publisher=Pappus OÜ |year=2022 |asin=B09TV5ZSMX}}</ref>

==Accolades==
{{Main|List of accolades received by Green Book{{!}}List of accolades received by ''Green Book''}}
''Green Book'' has received numerous award nominations. In addition to winning the [[Toronto International Film Festival People's Choice Award|People's Choice Award]] at the [[Toronto International Film Festival]] in September 2018,<ref name="tiff">[https://www.indiewire.com/2018/09/tiff-2018-awards-green-book-peoples-choice-1202004060/ "TIFF 2018 Awards: ‘Green Book’ Wins the People’s Choice Award, Upsetting ‘A Star Is Born’"]. ''[[IndieWire]]'', September 16, 2018.</ref> ''Green Book'' was nominated for five awards at the [[91st Academy Awards]], winning three awards for [[Academy Award for Best Picture|Best Picture]], [[Academy Award for Best Original Screenplay|Best Original Screenplay]] and [[Academy Award for Best Supporting Actor|Best Supporting Actor]] for [[Mahershala Ali]]. ''Green Book'' was the fifth film to win Best Picture without a Best Director nomination (after [[Wings (1927 film)|''Wings'']], ''[[Grand Hotel (1932 film)|Grand Hotel]]'', ''[[Driving Miss Daisy]]'' and ''[[Argo (2012 film)|Argo]]''). ''Green Book'' had five nominations at the [[76th Golden Globe Awards]], with the film winning [[Golden Globe Award for Best Motion Picture – Musical or Comedy|Best Motion Picture – Musical or Comedy]]. The [[National Board of Review Awards 2018|National Board of Review]] awarded it [[National Board of Review: Top Ten Films|Best Film]], and it was also recognized as one of the Top 10 films of the year by the [[American Film Institute Awards 2018|American Film Institute]].
