{{nihongo|'''''Be with You'''''|いま、会いにゆきます|Ima, Ai ni Yukimasu|lead=yes}} is a 2004 [[Japan]]ese [[Drama (film and television)|drama film]] based on a Japanese novel of the same name written by [[Takuji Ichikawa]].<ref name="cinematodayprofile">{{cite web |url=http://www.cinematoday.jp/movie/T0002514 |title=いま、会いにゆきます on cinematoday |accessdate=2011-10-15 |work= cinematoday| date=|language=Japanese}}</ref><ref>{{Cite web|url=https://www.redcircleauthors.com/our-authors/takuji-ichikawa/|title=Profile of Takuji Ichikawa|last=|first=|date=22 September 2019|website=Red Circle Authors|access-date=23 September 2019}}</ref> The film was adapted from the novel by [[Yoshikazu Okada (screenwriter)|Yoshikazu Okada]], and it was directed by [[Nobuhiro Doi]].<ref name="cinematodayprofile" /> It stars actress [[Yūko Takeuchi]] as Mio Aio and actor [[Shido Nakamura]] as Takumi Aio.<ref name="cinematodayprofile" />

''Be with You'' was first released at the 17th [[Tokyo International Film Festival]].<ref name="cinematoday20041028" /> It was subsequently released in Japanese cinemas on 30 October 2004.<ref name="cinematodayprofile" /> The film grossed a total of $46,616,207 from its screenings in Japan and overseas countries.<ref name="boxoficemojo" />
==Plot==
Mio Aio's death leaves her husband Takumi and six-year-old son Yuji to eke a living for themselves. Takumi is disorganized in household chores, suffers occasional fainting spells, and fears that his health could not fulfill his dead wife's happiness. Yuji overhears relatives gossiped that his own difficult delivery compromised Mio's health, and blames himself for his mother's death. Mio had left Yuji a picture book; in the book, Mio departs for a celestial body she calls "the Archive Star" but reappears in Japan during the following year's rainy season; turning the pages, Yuji eagerly awaits her return.

On a walk in the forest outside their house, Takumi and Yuji find a woman sheltered from the rain, and immediately accept her as Mio. She has no memory or sense of identity; she comes home to live with the father and son anyway. This new Mio asks Taku how they met and fell in love, and he recounts a tale of years of missed chances, beginning in high school and when she encouraged their marriage some time later. As the rainy season draws to a close, Yuji discovers the "time capsule" he hid with his mother before her death. Mio's diary is inside, and its version of the Mio-Taku romance holds the answers to the mystery.

==Release==
''Be with You'' was showcased at the 17th [[Tokyo International Film Festival]] on 27 October 2004.<ref name="cinematoday20041028">{{cite web |url=http://www.cinematoday.jp/page/L0000016 |script-title=ja:中村獅童、オレンジレンジとライブ？【第17回東京国際映画祭】 |accessdate=2011-10-15 |work= cinematoday| date=2004-10-28 |language=Japanese}}</ref> It was featured as one of the festival's special screenings.<ref name="cinematoday20041028" /> The singers for the theme song, Orange Range, also held a performance of the theme song at the showcase.<ref name="cinematoday20041028" />

==Theme song==
Okinawan alternative rock band [[Orange Range]] sang the theme song of this movie called ''[[Hana (Orange Range song)|Hana]]''.<ref name="cinematoday20041028" /> It was the top selling single of 2005.{{citation needed|date=July 2015}}
